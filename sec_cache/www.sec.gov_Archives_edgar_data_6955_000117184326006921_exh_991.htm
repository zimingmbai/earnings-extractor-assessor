EXHIBIT 99.1

Enerpac Tool Group Reports Fourth Quarter and Full-Year Fiscal 2026 Results; Introduces Fiscal 2027 Outlook

Highlights*

  • Fourth quarter net sales up 11% to $186 million, with organic growth of 10%1. Full-year net sales up 6% to $652 million, a record level of annual revenue since the relaunch of Enerpac Tool Group in 2019, with organic growth of 4%.
  • IT&S fourth quarter product sales up 11% and service sales up 9% organically year-over-year.
  • Fourth quarter net earnings of $24 million (13.1% of net sales), or $0.47 per diluted share, versus $28 million (16.8% of net sales), or $0.52, in the prior year; adjusted diluted EPS of $0.53 versus $0.52.
  • Full-year net earnings of $90 million (13.7% of net sales), or $1.72 per diluted share, versus $93 million (15.0% of net sales), or $1.70, in the prior year; adjusted diluted EPS of $1.87 versus $1.81.
  • Fourth quarter adjusted EBITDA of $42 million at a 22.6% margin; full-year adjusted EBITDA of $154 million at a 23.7% margin.
  • Full-year cash from operations of $130 million, up 17%, and free cash flow of $120 million, up 30% year-over-year. Net debt to adjusted EBITDA of 0.1x at year-end fiscal 2026, with $81 million returned to shareholders through share repurchases in fiscal 2026.
  • Completed the acquisition of SFE Group on October 28, 2026, as described in a separate press release issued today.

*This press release contains financial measures in accordance with U.S. Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (“GAAP”) in addition to non-GAAP financial measures. Reconciliations of the non-GAAP financial measures to the comparable GAAP measures are presented in the tables accompanying this release.

MILWAUKEE, Oct. 28, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Enerpac Tool Group Corp. (NYSE: EPAC) (the “Company” or “Enerpac”) today reported results for the fourth quarter and full fiscal year ended August 31, 2026.

“We closed fiscal 2026 with a strong fourth quarter, supported by broad-based product strength across the Americas, encouraging trends in our service business, and robust cash generation,” said Paul Sternlieb, President and Chief Executive Officer of Enerpac Tool Group. “With the SFE Group acquisition complete and a strong balance sheet, we have the financial flexibility to continue executing our disciplined capital allocation strategy.”

In the fourth quarter, net earnings declined year-over-year, primarily due to mix, higher incentive compensation, and investments for future growth. For the full year, net earnings declined to $89.7 million from $92.7 million, driven by lower service volume, higher incentive compensation, and investments for growth and productivity. Diluted EPS increased to $1.72 from $1.70, and adjusted diluted EPS increased to $1.87 from $1.81. Restructuring and M&A charges were $0.15 per diluted share in fiscal 2026, compared to $0.11 in fiscal 2025.

About Enerpac Tool Group
Enerpac Tool Group Corp. is a premier industrial tools, services, technology, and solutions provider serving a broad and diverse set of customers and end markets for mission-critical applications in more than 100 countries. Its tools and services make complex, often hazardous jobs possible safely and efficiently. The Company delivers mission-critical solutions across high-pressure hydraulics, precision-controlled force, lifting and positioning, pipe preparation, orbital welding and cutting, and on-site services for industrial customers worldwide. Founded in 1910, the Company is headquartered in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, and its common stock trades on the NYSE under the symbol EPAC. For more information, visit www.enerpactoolgroup.com.
