Century Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Business Updates

 

•CNTY-813, an iPSC-derived islet replacement therapy for T1D, presented new nonclinical data at the 62nd Annual Meeting of the European Association for the Study of Diabetes (EASD) and remains on track for IND submission in 4Q 2026; initial clinical data anticipated in 2H 2027

 

•CNTY-308, a CD19-targeted CAR-iT cell therapy with Allo-Evasion™ 5.0, remains on track to enter the clinic this year

 

•Cash runway into 1Q 2029 with cash, cash equivalents, and investments of $174.1 million as of September 30, 2026

 

PHILADELPHIA, November 12, 2026 –– Century Therapeutics, Inc. (‘Century’, NASDAQ: IPSC), a biotechnology company developing induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC)-derived cell therapies for autoimmune disease, including type 1 diabetes, and other severe diseases where engineered iPSCs have the unique potential to transform patients’ lives, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and recent business highlights.

 

“We remain focused on disciplined execution as we rapidly advance our two potentially transformational programs, including CNTY-813, which has the potential to be a functional cure for type 1 diabetes, toward the clinic over the coming months,” said Brent Pfeiffenberger, Pharm.D., Chief Executive Officer of Century Therapeutics. “This progress reflects the rigor of our science, the uniqueness of our expertise, and integrated capabilities to translate promising technologies into meaningful new treatment options for patients. With general alignment reached with the FDA for both programs, we remain on track this year for clinical entry of CNTY-308 and IND submission of CNTY-813, positioning us for an important transition to clinical execution in 2027.”

 

Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Highlights

 

Oral presentation at EASD 2026: CNTY-813 data further support advancement to the clinic (link to press release HERE)

 

•Allo-Evasion™ 5.0-edited CNTY-813 islets rapidly restored normoglycemia in streptozotocin-induced diabetic mice and maintained glycemic control until graft removal in a study lasting more than eleven months.

 

•All streptozotocin-induced diabetic mice administered CNTY-813 achieved normoglycemia, and CNTY-813 islets showed glucose-stimulated insulin secretion potency similar to that of primary islets.

 

 

 

 

•Five clinical-scale batches from Century’s GMP Master Cell Bank produced consistent final product profiles, with the final product exceeding purity criteria.

 

•In rats, CNTY-813 islets delivered via the liver portal vein were found to have engrafted in the liver at one month post-transplant and stained positive for insulin and chromogranin A, markers of β cells and endocrine cells, respectively.

 

•Mice transplanted with clinical-scale islets after 96 hours of cold storage normalized blood glucose within 30 days and maintained normoglycemia through three months of follow-up, thereby supporting the planned clinical supply chain strategy.

 

CNTY-813 IND submission remains on track for 4Q 2026

 

•Century expects to submit an IND for CNTY-813 in the fourth quarter of 2026.

 

•Following the pre-IND meeting, Century and the FDA reached general alignment on the nonclinical data package, the Phase 1 manufacturing process, and the proposed Phase 1/2 clinical trial design.

 

•Initial safety and early efficacy data from the first-in-human CNTY-813 study are anticipated in 2H 2027.

 

CNTY-308 clinical trial planned to initiate in 2026

 

•Century aligned with health authorities on nonclinical, manufacturing, and Phase 1 parameters. CNTY-308 is anticipated to enter the clinic in 2026.

 

•In nonclinical studies, CNTY-308 demonstrated functional comparability to autologous primary CAR-T cells, including in target-driven proliferation, cytokine secretion, and persistence. Autologous CAR-T cells have shown clinical proof of concept across several autoimmune diseases.

 

Joseph Truitt appointed to Board of Directors

 

Century announced the appointment of Joseph Truitt to its Board of Directors. Mr. Truitt is Chief Executive Officer of iECURE, Inc. and Chairman of the Board of Larimar Therapeutics, and previously served as Chief Executive Officer of BioSpecifics Technologies Corp. and Achillion Pharmaceuticals.

 

 

 

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

•Cash Position: Cash, cash equivalents, and investments were $174.1 million as of September 30, 2026, as compared to $117.1 million as of December 31, 2025. The company estimates its cash, cash equivalents, and investments as of September 30, 2026 will support operations into 1Q 2029.

 

•Research and Development (R&D) Expenses: R&D expenses were $18.1 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $22.5 million for the same period in 2025. The decrease was primarily the result of a reduction in personnel and a reduction in facility costs as a result of our previously announced portfolio prioritization.

 

•General and Administrative (G&A) Expenses: G&A expenses were $7.1 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $6.8 million for the same period in 2025.

 

•Net Loss: Net loss was $23.5 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to a net loss of $34.4 million for the same period in 2025.

 

About Century Therapeutics

 

Century Therapeutics (NASDAQ: IPSC) is a biotechnology company advancing a pipeline of induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC)-derived cell therapies with the potential to meaningfully address autoimmune disease, including type 1 diabetes, and other severe diseases where engineered iPSCs have the unique potential to transform patients’ lives. Century’s therapies are derived from its iPSC cell foundry and leverage its novel immune evasion engineering technology, Allo-Evasion™. Century believes its approach to developing off-the-shelf cell therapies will expand patient access and provide advantages over existing cell therapies which will ultimately advance the course of care. For more information on Century Therapeutics, please visit www.centurytx.com and connect with us on LinkedIn.
