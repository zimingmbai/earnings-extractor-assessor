EXHIBIT 99.1

Aligos Therapeutics Reports Recent Business Progress and Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

SOUTH SAN FRANCISCO, Calif., Nov. 06, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Aligos Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: ALGS, “Aligos”), a clinical stage biotechnology company focused on improving patient outcomes through best-in-class therapies for liver and viral diseases, today reported recent business progress and financial results for the third quarter 2026.

“This past quarter we continued to advance our Phase 2 B-SUPREME study of pevifoscorvir sodium, which remains on track for the topline data readout in late Q3 2027,” stated Lawrence Blatt, Ph.D., M.B.A., Chairman, President, and Chief Executive Officer of Aligos Therapeutics. “With enrollment complete and 70 participants having completed 48 weeks of treatment, the study continues to progress toward the anticipated topline data readout. We remain enthusiastic about pevifoscorvir sodium’s potential, as well as our ASO ALG-170675, which is currently in a Phase 1 study in China with our partner Amoytop. With these recent developments, we believe we are one step closer to our goal of bringing effective therapies to people living with chronic HBV infection.”

Recent Business Progress

Pipeline Updates

      Pevifoscorvir sodium: Potential first-/best-in-class small molecule CAM-E for chronic hepatitis B virus (HBV) infection

  • The Phase 2 B-SUPREME study (NCT06963710) has completed enrollment with 131 participants enrolled in the HBeAg+ cohort (Part 1a) and 114 participants enrolled in the HBeAg- cohort (Part 2a).
  • Topline data for both the HBeAg+ and HBeAg- cohorts are expected in late Q3 2027.

ALG-170675: Potential best-in-class antisense oligonucleotide (ASO) for chronic hepatitis B virus (HBV) infection

  • Our partner Amoytop advanced ALG-170675 into Phase 1 clinical studies. Current costs for development in China are being funded by Amoytop, who maintain rights in Greater China.
  • Under our agreement with Amoytop, Aligos may utilize data from these studies for regulatory filings and other purposes. Each party is responsible for their own development costs.

Financial Results for the Three Months Ended September 30, 2026

Cash, cash equivalents and investments totaled $31.1 million as of September 30, 2026, compared with $77.8 million as of December 31, 2025. Our cash and cash equivalents are expected to provide sufficient funding of planned operations into the first quarter of 2027.

Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $21.9 million or basic and diluted net loss per common share of $(2.09), compared to net loss of $31.5 million or basic and diluted net loss per common share of $(3.04), for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

Research and development (R&D) expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were $18.9 million, compared with $23.9 million for the same period of 2025. The decrease was primarily due to an decrease in third-party expenses for the pevifoscorvir sodium Phase 2 clinical trial. Total R&D stock-based compensation expense incurred for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $0.6 million, compared with $0.8 million for the same period of 2025.

General and administrative (G&A) expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were $5.9 million, compared with $5.2 million for the same period of 2025. The increase in G&A expenses for this comparative period is primarily due to a increase in legal and other related expenses. Total G&A stock-based compensation expense incurred for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $0.6 million, compared with $0.7 million for the same period of 2025.

Interest and other income, net, was income of $0.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared with income of $1.1 million for the same period in 2025.

Change in fair value of 2023 common warrants for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was income of $0.2 million compared with a loss of $4.2 million for the same period of 2025.

About Aligos

Aligos Therapeutics, Inc. (NASDAQ: ALGS) is a clinical stage biotechnology company founded with the mission to improve patient outcomes by developing best-in-class therapies for the treatment of liver and viral diseases. Aligos applies its science driven approach and deep R&D expertise to advance its purpose-built pipeline of therapeutics for high unmet medical needs such as chronic hepatitis B virus (HBV) infection.
