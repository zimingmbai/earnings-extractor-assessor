Carter’s, Inc. Reports Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results
•Net sales $752 million vs. $758 million in Q3 2025, decrease of 0.7%
•U.S. Retail comparable sales increased 0.4%
•Operating income $56 million vs. $29 million in Q3 2025
•Adjusted operating income $57 million vs. $39 million in Q3 2025, growth of 45%
•Diluted EPS $1.00 vs. $0.32 in Q3 2025
•Adjusted diluted EPS $1.03 vs. $0.74 in Q3 2025, growth of 39%
•Returned $27 million to shareholders through dividends in the first three quarters of fiscal 2026
•Announced strategic evolution of leadership structure; see separate press release issued today
•Updates full year 2026 outlook
ATLANTA, October 30, 2026 — Carter’s, Inc. (NYSE:CRI), North America’s largest and most-enduring apparel company exclusively for babies and young children, today reported its third quarter fiscal 2026 results.
“I am pleased with the significant progress of the company’s profitability improvement initiatives in the third quarter, especially in light of delivering slight growth in U.S. Retail comparative sales and overall net sales that were slightly above our outlook amid an uneven macroeconomic environment. As the quarter unfolded, the U.S. Retail team quickly and effectively worked to adjust our marketing and promotional efforts in response to the challenges to help deliver these results. Our solid third quarter performance is currently expected to contribute to another year of growth in net sales, U.S. Retail comparable sales, and operating profit for fiscal 2026, even as we moderate our revenue outlook to reflect the continued pressure on our consumers from the ongoing economic and geopolitical uncertainty,” said Sharon Price John, Chief Executive Officer & President. “Separately, over the past several months in my new role, we have continued to define a strategic framework based on our tenets of keeping the consumer at the center of all that we do, leveraging the power of our market-leading brands, and utilizing our meaningful data and insights to drive innovation and decision making while staying focused on delivering profitable sales. Indicative of our intent to take significant and rapid steps to improve the business, for the first time in 25 years, during the quarter we successfully launched a comprehensive initiative for our

flagship Carter’s brand, including a reimagined logo and brand promise of “let every child's light shine”, with the goal of increasing appeal and relevance to today’s generation of parents. Separately, we announced the evolution of our leadership structure designed to increase synergy, improve processes, and better align with our go-forward strategy. While early, I’m encouraged by our progress and believe we are well positioned to continue to unlock this iconic company's potential to deliver meaningful shareholder returns over time,” Ms. John concluded.

Consolidated Results
Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 compared to Third Quarter Fiscal 2025
Net sales decreased $5.4 million, or 0.7%, to $752.5 million, compared to $757.8 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2025. Net sales increased 4.5% in the International segment, were comparable to prior year in U.S. Retail, and decreased 3.8% in U.S. Wholesale. U.S. Retail comparable net sales increased 0.4%. Changes in foreign currency exchange rates used for translation had a favorable effect on consolidated net sales of approximately $0.9 million, or 0.1% in the third quarter of fiscal 2026, as compared to the third quarter of fiscal 2025.
Operating income increased $26.7 million, or 91.7%, to $55.8 million, compared to $29.1 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2025, reflecting higher realized pricing, benefits from productivity initiatives, and the non-recurrence of organizational restructuring and operating model improvement costs in the prior year period. These benefits were partially offset by increased marketing investments as well as general cost inflation. Operating margin increased to 7.4%, compared to 3.8% in the prior year.
Adjusted operating income (a non-GAAP measure) increased $17.6 million, or 44.6% to $56.9 million, compared to $39.4 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2025, principally due to higher realized pricing and benefits from productivity initiatives, which more than offset increased marketing investments and general cost inflation. Adjusted operating margin increased to 7.6%, compared to 5.2% in the prior year period.
Net income was $36.6 million, or $1.00 per diluted share, compared to $11.6 million, or $0.32 per diluted share, in the third quarter of fiscal 2025.
Adjusted net income (a non-GAAP measure) was $37.5 million, compared to $26.8 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2025. Adjusted earnings per diluted share (a non-GAAP measure) was $1.03, compared to $0.74 in the prior-year quarter.

First Three Quarters of Fiscal 2026 compared to First Three Quarters of Fiscal 2025
Net sales increased $76.1 million, or 3.9%, to $2.05 billion, compared to $1.97 billion in the first three quarters of fiscal 2025, reflecting growth in each segment. Net sales in the International, U.S. Retail, and U.S. Wholesale segments grew 6.8%, 4.5%, and 1.8%, respectively. U.S. Retail comparable net sales increased 5.0%. Changes in foreign currency exchange rates used for translation had a favorable effect on consolidated net sales of approximately $8.9 million, or 0.5% in the first three quarters of fiscal 2026, as compared to the first three quarters of fiscal 2025.
Operating income increased $164.8 million to $224.0 million, compared to $59.2 million in the first three quarters of fiscal 2025, reflecting a recovery of previously paid import duties, benefits from productivity initiatives, and the non-recurrence of organizational restructuring and operating model improvement costs in the prior year period. These benefits were partially offset by incremental tariff costs, increased marketing investments, and general cost inflation. Operating margin increased to 10.9%, compared to 3.0% in the prior year period.
Adjusted operating income (a non-GAAP measure) increased $17.0 million, or 19.6% to $103.5 million, compared to $86.5 million in the first three quarters of fiscal 2025, principally due to higher net sales and the benefits from productivity initiatives, which more than offset incremental tariff costs, increased marketing investments, and general cost inflation. Adjusted operating margin increased to 5.0%, compared to 4.4% in the prior year period.
Net income was $155.9 million, or $4.27 per diluted share, compared to $27.6 million, or $0.75 per diluted share, in the first three quarters of fiscal 2025.
Adjusted net income (a non-GAAP measure) was $61.2 million, compared to $56.9 million in the first three quarters of fiscal 2025. Adjusted earnings per diluted share (a non-GAAP measure) was $1.68, compared to adjusted earnings per diluted share of $1.57 in the first three quarters of fiscal 2025.
Net cash provided by operations in the first three quarters of fiscal 2026 was $101.8 million, compared to net cash used in operations of $136.3 million in the first three quarters of fiscal 2025. The improved operating cash flow was primarily driven by the receipt of a refund of previously paid import duties and improved working capital.

About Carter’s, Inc.
Carter’s, Inc. is North America’s largest and most-enduring apparel company exclusively for babies and young children. The Company’s core brands are Carter’s and OshKosh B’gosh, iconic and among the sector’s most trusted names. These brands are sold through more than 1,000 Company-operated stores in the United States, Canada, and Mexico, and online at www.carters.com, www.oshkosh.com, www.cartersoshkosh.ca, and www.carters.com.mx. Carter’s also is the largest supplier of baby and young children’s apparel to North America’s biggest retailers. The Company’s Child of Mine brand is available exclusively at Walmart, its Just One You brand is available at Target, and its Simple Joys brand is available on Amazon.com. The Company’s emerging brands include Little Planet, crafted with organic fabrics and sustainable materials, Otter Avenue, a toddler-focused apparel brand, and Skip Hop, baby essentials from tubs to toys. Carter’s is headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia. Additional information may be found at www.carters.com.

The use of EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA instead of net income or cash flows from operations has limitations as an analytical tool, and should not be considered in isolation, or as a substitute for analysis of the Company’s results as reported under GAAP. While EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA and similar measures are frequently used as measures of operations and the ability to meet debt service requirements, these terms are not necessarily comparable to other similarly titled captions of other companies due to the potential inconsistencies in the method of calculation. EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA do not reflect the impact of earnings or charges resulting from matters that the Company considers not to be indicative of its ongoing operations. Because of these limitations, EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered as discretionary cash available to the Company for working capital, debt service and other purposes.
