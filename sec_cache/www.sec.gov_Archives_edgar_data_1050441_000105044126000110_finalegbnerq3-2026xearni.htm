PRESS RELEASE FOR EAGLE BANCORP, INC. IMMEDIATE RELEASE CONTACT: Eric R. Newell October 21, 2026 240.497.1796 EAGLE BANCORP, INC. ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS BETHESDA, MD, Eagle Bancorp, Inc. ("Eagle" or the "Company") (NASDAQ: EGBN), the Bethesda-based holding company for EagleBank (the "Bank"), one of the largest community banks in the Washington D.C. area, reported its unaudited results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. Eagle reported net income of $24.2 million or $0.79 per share for the third quarter 2026, compared to $6.9 million or $0.23 per share for the second quarter of 2026. This $17.3 million increase in net income is primarily due to a $18.6 million lower provision for credit losses, compared to the prior quarter. In the third quarter, net interest income decreased $1.0 million, noninterest income decreased $1.2 million, while noninterest expense increased $3.1 million. Pre-provision net revenue ("PPNR")1 declined in the third quarter to $23.8 million compared to $29.1 million for the prior quarter reflecting higher noninterest expense, lower noninterest income, and slightly lower net interest income. "This quarter provides tangible evidence of progress in executing on our strategy. Criticized and classified loans declined, net charge-offs moderated significantly, funding costs improved, and total commercial deposits grew sequentially. We also recognize that the increase in nonperforming assets, driven primarily by one significant relationship, means our asset quality work is not complete. We remain focused on resolving problem assets, strengthening relationship-based funding, and translating the capabilities of this franchise into consistent, profitable growth," said Steve Curley, President and Chief Executive Officer of the Company. Curley continued, “We also took important steps to strengthen Eagle’s growth capabilities by establishing a commercial banking presence in Baltimore, adding experienced leaders to our commercial real estate business, and appointing a new Chief People Officer. We are bringing together the talent, market coverage, and organizational leadership needed to support disciplined, profitable growth.” Additionally, the Company is announcing today a cash dividend in the amount of $0.01 per share. The cash dividend will be payable on November 17, 2026 to shareholders of record on November 6, 2026. 1 1 A reconciliation of non-GAAP financial measures and the nearest GAAP measures is provided in the GAAP Reconciliation to Non-GAAP Financial Measures tables that accompany this document.

 
Third Quarter of 2026 Key Elements • The Company announces today the declaration of a common stock dividend of $0.01 per share. • Total C&I loans (including owner-occupied) increased $536.5 million, or 19.54%, year- over-year, and period end C&I deposit growth totaled $111.1 million, or 6.43%. Performing office coverage2 was 4.86% at quarter-end; as compared to 7.22% at the prior quarter- end, as the CRE office portfolio decreased and asset quality indicators improved. • Non-performing assets increased by $52.9 million to $166.0 million as of September 30, 2026, representing 1.75% of total assets, compared to $113.1 million, representing 1.17% of total assets as of June 30, 2026. During the quarter, non-performing loan inflows totaled $73.7 million, 76.0% of which resulted from a single loan migration. Charge offs during the quarter declined due to less disposition activity related to classified assets. • The net interest margin ("NIM") increased to 2.60% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 2.52% for the prior quarter, primarily driven by improved funding mix as reduced brokered deposit usage lowered cost of funds. This improvement was partially offset by lower interest income from declines in loan balances. • At quarter-end, the common equity ratio, tangible common equity ratio1, and common equity tier 1 capital (to risk-weighted assets) ratio were 12.24%, 12.24%, and 14.99%, respectively.
