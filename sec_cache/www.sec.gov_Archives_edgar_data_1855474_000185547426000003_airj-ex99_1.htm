AirJoule Technologies Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results and Provides Preliminary 2027 BitSink Outlook

•
Completes BitSink Acquisition and Sharpens Commercial Focus on Highest-Value Applications for AirJouleTM Technology
•
Provides Preliminary 2027 BitSink Revenue Outlook of $15 Million to $20 Million
•
First AirJoule Prime System Delivered to Data4 Data Center in France

 

Ronan, Mont., November 16, 2026 – AirJoule Technologies Corporation (NASDAQ: AIRJ) (“AIRJ” or the “Company”) today reported financial and operating results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“This quarter marks an important step in AirJoule Technologies’ evolution. We expanded our commercial capabilities through the acquisition of BitSink, strengthened our market access through partnerships like Kubota, and sharpened our AirJoule product roadmap around our two highest-value platforms: Prime for water generation and Core DH for dehumidification,” said Matt Jore, Founder and Chief Executive Officer of AirJoule Technologies.

“BitSink’s $15 million in signed purchase orders provides greater near-term revenue visibility, while our AirJoule products are moving through a more defined path to commercialization. We are deploying systems in customer environments, measuring real-world performance, and working to validate the economics that will support broader adoption. This progress is helping us focus on the markets where we can create the greatest customer value, and we head into 2027 with more commercial traction than at any point in our history.”

Third Quarter and Recent Highlights

•
Acquired BitSink. AIRJ completed its acquisition of BitSink on September 10, 2026, adding data center infrastructure capabilities, manufacturing capacity, customer relationships and $15 million of signed purchase orders scheduled for delivery over the next year. BitSink recently moved into a new 65,000-square-foot manufacturing facility in Chesnee, South Carolina, and is setting up equipment and operations to meet expected demand in 2027. Founder Stan Dyshko continues to lead the business as Chief Operating Officer of BitSink.
•
Delivered the First AirJoule Prime System to Data4. AIRJ delivered the system to Data4’s Marcoussis data center campus in France. Installation and commissioning are planned over the coming weeks, with operation expected to begin by the end of 2026. The deployment is being done in collaboration with the Net Zero Innovation Hub for Data Centers, a technology and sustainability consortium led by Data4, Microsoft, Google, APL, Danfoss, Vertiv, and Schneider Electric.
•
Announced Exclusive Sales Agreement with Kubota Corporation (“Kubota”) for Residential Development and Commenced Initial Pilot Projects. AIRJ commenced its Kubota partnership with the delivery of an AirJoule Core AWG system to Irvine, California, where it is being used to demonstrate the AirJoule technology for Kubota’s residential developer customers. The system was commissioned in October 2026, and a second AirJoule Core AWG unit has been delivered to Corpus Christi, Texas, where commissioning is expected in December 2026.
•
Sharpened AirJoule Product Roadmap Around Prime Water Generation and Core DH Dehumidification. AIRJ focused its AirJoule product development and commercialization efforts on two purpose-built platforms: Prime for atmospheric water generation and Core DH for energy-efficient dehumidification. This strategy builds on learnings from earlier Core AWG deployments, which will continue to support customer demonstrations and performance validation. The refined roadmap is designed to improve product economics and accelerate progress toward repeatable commercial adoption.

BitSink Acquisition

•
Acquisition Completed: On September 10, 2026, AIRJ acquired 100% of BitSink, a U.S.-based designer and manufacturer of modular data center infrastructure, liquid cooling systems and electrical equipment for artificial intelligence (“AI”) and high-performance computing (“HPC”) workloads. Upfront consideration was $18 million in cash and $9 million in AIRJ common stock, with no debt. The transaction includes up to $40 million of additional consideration, payable solely in AIRJ common stock and tied to BitSink revenue milestones of $30.0 million in 2027 and $63.6 million in 2028, aligning the majority of potential consideration with future performance.
•
Strategic Rationale: AI and HPC are changing data center infrastructure requirements, and cooling, power infrastructure and water have become three of the most critical constraints on new capacity. BitSink adds an established and profitable operating business with more than 220 megawatts (“MW”) of cooling and power equipment deployed across North America with 99% uptime, approximately 40% historical gross margins, a U.S. manufacturing base that includes a 65,000-square-foot facility in Chesnee, South Carolina, and $15 million of signed purchase orders to be delivered over the next year.
•
Positioned in Modular AI Infrastructure: BitSink’s integrated modular blocks, dry coolers, switchboards and power distribution units serve modular and smaller AI and HPC sites, which can be brought online faster and face fewer regulatory and permitting restrictions compared to large greenfield campuses.

1

•
NEXUS 3.0 Modular AI Infrastructure: BitSink’s $15 million of purchase orders are for NEXUS 3.0, its modular AI infrastructure block. Each block delivers 3.0 MW of total power and supports 2.5 MW of critical IT load across liquid-cooled racks designed for NVIDIA GB300 and Cerebras CS-3 systems, with flexibility for other AI architectures. Blocks combine liquid cooling, heat rejection, power distribution, UPS and on-site battery storage, will be built and tested in Chesnee before shipment, and use sealed, closed cooling loops that require no ongoing water supply.
•
Complementary Water and Cooling Technology: BitSink’s closed-loop liquid cooling systems reject the type of low-grade waste heat that AirJoule Prime uses to produce pure distilled water. Together, the two systems represent a path toward water-neutral, and potentially water-positive, liquid-cooled AI infrastructure.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

BitSink is a wholly owned subsidiary whose results have been consolidated into AIRJ’s financial statements since the September 10, 2026 acquisition date.

AirJoule, LLC, AIRJ’s 50/50 joint venture with GE Vernova, is accounted for under the equity method. Accordingly, the joint venture’s revenue and operating expenses are not consolidated into AIRJ’s revenue and operating-expense lines.

•
Net operating expenses for AIRJ were $5.5 million, including $2.7 million of non-cash stock-based compensation and $0.7 million of BitSink acquisition-related costs.
•
AIRJ contributed $3.5 million to the joint venture during the quarter to support productization, manufacturing and commercial-deployment activities.
•
AirJoule joint venture operating expenses totaled $4.7 million.
•
At September 30, 2026, AIRJ and the AirJoule joint venture held a combined cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash of $31.4 million, with no debt.

Outlook

•
AIRJ reaffirmed its prior guidance for $27 million to $28 million of combined AIRJ and AirJoule joint venture cash spending in 2026, excluding BitSink.
•
AIRJ’s preliminary 2027 revenue outlook for BitSink is $15 million to $20 million, supported by $15 million in signed purchase orders and anticipated additional customer demand.
•
AIRJ also expects the BitSink segment to generate neutral to positive free cash flow in 2027.

Appointment of Timothy N. White to Board of Directors

Timothy N. White, Senior Vice President, Engineering & Technology at Carrier Global Corporation, has joined AIRJ’s Board of Directors, succeeding Ajay Agrawal. White has more than 30 years of experience leading engineering organizations, development programs and industrial businesses at Carrier, General Electric and Raytheon Technologies.

“We thank Ajay for his contributions to AIRJ and our relationship with Carrier,” said Pat Eilers, Executive Chairman of AirJoule Technologies. “Tim’s engineering, product and manufacturing experience will be valuable as we advance our products toward commercial scale.”

About AirJoule Technologies Corporation

AirJoule Technologies Corporation (NASDAQ: AIRJ) is a water and cooling technology platform that seeks to free the world of its water and energy constraints. The company is commercializing its proprietary AirJoule™ technology for atmospheric water generation and dehumidification through a joint venture with GE Vernova. AirJoule Technologies also owns BitSink, a U.S. designer and manufacturer of modular data center infrastructure, liquid cooling systems and electrical equipment for AI and high-performance computing workloads. For more information, visit https://airjouletech.com.
