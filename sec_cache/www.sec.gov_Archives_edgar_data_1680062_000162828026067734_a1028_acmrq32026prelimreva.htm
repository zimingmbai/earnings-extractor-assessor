ACM Research Announces Preliminary Unaudited Revenue and Shipments for the Third Quarter 2026

Coincides with Release of ACM Shanghai Third Quarter 2026 Results

FREMONT, Calif., Oct 28, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – ACM Research, Inc. (“ACM”) (NASDAQ: ACMR), a leading supplier of wafer and panel processing solutions for semiconductor and advanced packaging applications, today announces expectations for preliminary unaudited revenue and total shipments for the third quarter of 2026. Today’s release coincides with the as-scheduled release of unaudited financial results by ACM Research (Shanghai), Inc., ACM’s principal operating subsidiary (“ACM Shanghai”), to the Shanghai Stock Exchange website [link to China Disclosure].
ACM will discuss its full financial results for the third quarter 2026 and its revenue outlook for the remainder of the year on its earnings call on Thursday, November 5, 2026, at 8 a.m. Eastern Time (9 p.m. China Time).
ACM announces the following:
•preliminary unaudited revenue for the third quarter of 2026 is expected to be in the range of $360 million to $365 million, which would represent year-to-year growth of 34% to 36%.
•preliminary total shipments are expected to be in the range of $335 million to $340 million, which would represent year-to-year growth of 27% to 29%.
Actual unaudited third quarter 2026 results are subject to the completion of ACM’s quarter end closing procedures and review by ACM’s independent registered public accounting firm.
As of June 30, 2026, ACM owned a 73.2% equity interest in ACM Shanghai, and a substantial majority of ACM’s consolidated revenue and net income is contributed by ACM Shanghai. The stand-alone financial results of ACM Shanghai are reported in RMB as prepared in accordance with Chinese generally accepted accounting principles, and those results will differ, potentially materially, from ACM’s consolidated revenue and net profit for the period, which will reflect additional financial and operational items and will be prepared in U.S. dollars in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles.
