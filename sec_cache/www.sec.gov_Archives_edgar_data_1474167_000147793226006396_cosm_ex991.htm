CHICAGO, IL / ACCESS Newswire / October 19, 2026 / Cosmos Health Inc. (“Cosmos Health” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: COSM), a diversified, vertically integrated global healthcare group, today announced that, based on its current growth trajectory, it expects U.S. revenue of approximately $15 million for the fourth quarter of 2026, with gross profit of approximately $10.5 million. Subscriptions are expected to generate more than three-quarters of fourth quarter U.S. revenue, and momentum toward this outlook continues to build: as of October 19, 2026, active U.S. subscriptions reached 8,940, up 42% from the 6,287 announced on October 14, 2026.

 

 

 

 

 

Fourth Quarter 2026 U.S. Outlook

 

Based on its current growth trajectory, Cosmos Health expects its U.S. revenue for the fourth quarter ending December 31, 2026 to reach approximately $15 million, with gross profit of approximately $10.5 million. The outlook reflects the Company’s U.S. online direct-to-consumer business and is supported by a significantly expanded marketing budget for the fourth quarter.

 

Revenue is expected to accelerate through the quarter, with December expected to be the strongest month. New subscriptions and renewals are expected to build on each other month after month.

Subscriptions are expected to generate approximately $11.5 million, or more than three-quarters, of fourth quarter U.S. revenue. This includes approximately $8.2 million from first orders placed by new subscribers and approximately $3.3 million from renewals of subscriptions started during the quarter. One-time purchases are expected to contribute the remaining approximately $3.5 million.

 

Renewals are expected to grow each month as successive cohorts of subscribers renew, steadily increasing the recurring portion of U.S. revenue.

 

 
2

 

 

Gross Profit

 

At an expected gross margin of approximately 70%, the Company expects fourth quarter U.S. gross profit of approximately $10.5 million. The Company believes this margin profile reflects the efficiency of its direct-to-consumer model.

 

Key Growth Drivers and Expected Growth

 

The Company’s outlook is supported by several drivers: increased marketing investment in its U.S. direct-to-consumer platform, expanded digital customer acquisition, rising brand awareness, a broadening product offering, growing repeat purchases, and strong subscription adoption at checkout. Each new subscription adds to a recurring base, as subscribers renew month after month and average approximately 2.4 product units per subscription.

 

These drivers are already visible in recent results, and momentum is building further. As of October 19, 2026, the Company’s platform had tracked 14,624 U.S. online checkout orders, up 34% from the 10,881 announced on October 14, 2026, and the Company had 8,940 active U.S. subscriptions, up 42% from 6,287, with monthly subscription value at standard renewal pricing of $1,325,338, up 44% from $919,945 over the same five days. Overall, since the inception of the Company’s subscription platform, 65.2% of U.S. online checkout orders have included a subscription, up from 60.5% previously reported, rising to more than 85% of daily orders in recent days.

 

As a result, the Company expects U.S. daily sales to more than triple during the quarter, from $60,000 at its start to more than $200,000 by year-end, and expects active U.S. subscriptions to exceed 40,000 by year-end, more than four times current levels.

 

CEO Commentary

 

Greg Siokas, CEO of Cosmos Health, stated: “Our U.S. business has strong momentum entering the fourth quarter, and it is building further. In the five days since our last update, active subscriptions grew 42% to 8,940 and their monthly value rose 44% to more than $1.3 million, which reinforces our confidence in the outlook. We expect approximately $15 million in U.S. revenue and $10.5 million in gross profit for the quarter, with daily sales exceeding $200,000 and more than 40,000 active subscriptions by year-end.

 

“What matters most to us is the quality of that revenue. Every month, a new cohort of subscribers renews and adds to a recurring revenue base, at strong margins. We look forward to keeping investors updated on our progress in the weeks ahead.”

 

Note: The fourth quarter 2026 U.S. revenue, gross margin, gross profit, revenue mix, daily sales and subscription expectations are based on the Company’s current growth trajectory and internal assumptions. Subscription and order figures are as reported by the Company’s platform for its U.S. online stores, are preliminary and unaudited, and may differ from amounts reported in the Company’s financial statements. Monthly subscription value is calculated at standard renewal pricing, is an operating metric, is not a measure of revenue under U.S. GAAP and does not represent revenue earned to date.

About Cosmos Health Inc.

Cosmos Health Inc. (NASDAQ: COSM), incorporated in 2009 in Nevada, is a diversified, vertically integrated global healthcare group. The Company owns a portfolio of proprietary pharmaceutical and nutraceutical brands, including Sky Premium Life®, Mediterranation®, bio-bebe®, C-Sept® and C-Scrub®. Through its subsidiary Cana Laboratories S.A., licensed under European Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) and certified by the European Medicines Agency (EMA), it manufactures pharmaceuticals, food supplements, cosmetics, biocides, and medical devices within the European Union. Cosmos Health also distributes a broad line of pharmaceuticals and parapharmaceuticals, including branded generics and OTC medications, to retail pharmacies and wholesale distributors through its subsidiaries in Greece and the UK. Furthermore, the Company has established R&D partnerships targeting major health disorders such as obesity, diabetes, and cancer, enhanced by artificial intelligence drug repurposing technologies, and focuses on the R&D of novel patented nutraceuticals, specialized root extracts, proprietary complex generics, and innovative OTC products. Cosmos Health has also entered the telehealth space through the acquisition of ZipDoctor, Inc., based in Texas, USA. With a global distribution platform, the Company is currently expanding throughout Europe, Asia, and North America, and has offices and distribution centers in Thessaloniki and Athens, Greece, and in Harlow, UK. More information is available at www.cosmoshealthinc.com,  www.skypremiumlife.com, www.cana.gr, www.zipdoctor.co, www.cloudscreen.gr, as well as LinkedIn and X.
