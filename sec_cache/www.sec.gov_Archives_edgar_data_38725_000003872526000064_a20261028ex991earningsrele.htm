FRANKLIN ELECTRIC REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•Consolidated net sales of $633.6 million, an increase of 9% to the prior year
•Net sales increased across all three segments: 5% in Water Systems, 15% in Energy Systems, and 15% in Distribution
•Operating income was $86.5 million, an increase of 2% to the prior year, with operating margin of 13.7%; Adjusted operating income2 was $95.4 million
•GAAP fully diluted earnings per share (EPS) was $1.35, an increase of 265%; Adjusted diluted EPS1 was $1.50, an increase of 15%

Fort Wayne, IN – October 28, 2026 – Franklin Electric Co., Inc. today announced its third quarter financial results for fiscal year 2026.

Third quarter 2026 net sales were $633.6 million compared to third quarter 2025 net sales of $581.7 million. Third quarter 2026 operating income was $86.5 million, compared to third quarter 2025 operating income of $85.1 million. Third quarter 2026 diluted EPS was $1.35, compared to third quarter 2025 EPS of $0.37. Third quarter adjusted diluted EPS1 was $1.50, compared to 2025 third quarter adjusted diluted EPS1 of $1.31.

1 – Adjusted diluted EPS is a non-GAAP measure. Please refer to our Note Regarding Non-GAAP Financial Measures below for further explanation as well as the final page of this release for a reconciliation of non-GAAP measures to the appropriate GAAP measure.

“Our team delivered strong third quarter revenue growth through a combination of favorable pricing, contributions from recent acquisitions, and healthy customer activity across much of the portfolio. We were pleased with the early contribution from Cat Pumps as our teams remain focused on executing our integration playbook while serving customers at a high level,” commented Joe Ruzynski, Franklin Electric’s CEO.

“Although we experienced margin pressure from higher material and freight costs, we continue to see encouraging order trends and strong customer engagement across many of our businesses. We remain focused on disciplined pricing, productivity initiatives, and operational execution throughout our global manufacturing and distribution network. With a diversified portfolio, strong balance sheet, and multiple opportunities to drive growth, we believe Franklin Electric is well positioned to continue creating long-term value for shareholders.”


Segment Summaries

Water Systems net sales were $352.5 million in the third quarter, an increase of $15.9 million or 5 percent compared to the third quarter of 2025. The incremental sales were driven by the sales impact of recent acquisitions, favorable foreign exchange, and price realization. Water Systems operating income in the third quarter of 2026 was $58.9 million, a decrease of 2 percent over third quarter 2025 operating income of $60.2 million. Water Systems had $0.3 million of restructuring expenses and $0.8 million in acquisition-related costs and impact of selling acquired inventory after markup to fair value as part of acquisition accounting in the third quarter with adjusted operating income2 of $60.0 million, a decrease of 2 percent over the third quarter of 2025 adjusted operating income2 of $61.3 million.




Distribution net sales were $226.1 million, an increase of $28.8 million or 15 percent compared to the third quarter of 2025. Sales increases were driven by higher volumes, the incremental sales impact of recent acquisitions, and price realization. The Distribution segment operating income in the third quarter 2026 was $20.6 million, an increase of 26 percent over third quarter 2025 operating income of $16.3 million.

Energy Systems net sales were $91.7 million in the third quarter 2026, an increase of $11.7 million or 15 percent compared to the third quarter 2025. Sales increases were driven by the sales impact of recent acquisitions, higher price realization and volumes. Energy Systems operating income in the third quarter of 2026 was $23.7 million compared to the third quarter of 2025 operating income of $25.4 million. Energy Systems operating income in the third quarter of 2026 included $4.7 million acquisition-related costs and $3.1 million impact of marking up acquired inventory to fair value as part of acquisition accounting compared to $0.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. Energy Systems adjusted operating income2 was $31.5 million, an increase of 24 percent over the third quarter of 2025 adjusted operating income2 of $25.4 million.


2 – Adjusted operating income is a non-GAAP measure. Please refer to our Note Regarding Non-GAAP Financial Measures below for further explanation as well as the final page of this release for a reconciliation of non-GAAP measures to the appropriate GAAP measure.

2026 Guidance

The Company is raising its guidance for full year 2026 net sales to be in the range of $2.23 billion to $2.30 billion and full year 2026 Adjusted Diluted EPS to be in the range of $4.62 to $4.82.

About Franklin Electric

Franklin Electric is a global leader in the production and marketing of systems and components for the movement of water and energy. Recognized as a technical leader in its products and services, Franklin Electric serves customers worldwide in residential, commercial, agricultural, industrial, municipal and fueling applications.
