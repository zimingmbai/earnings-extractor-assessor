TOMI Environmental Solutions, Inc. Reports Preliminary Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Company Delivers 24% Q3 Revenue Growth and Returns to Positive Operating Income


FREDERICK, Md., Nov. 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TOMI Environmental Solutions, Inc. (NASDAQ: TOMZ), a global provider of disinfection and decontamination essentials through its premier Binary Ionization Technology® (BIT™) platform, today announced preliminary financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, and provided an update on strategic initiatives.

 

Select financial and operational achievements for the quarter are as follows:

 

●  Q3 Revenue Increased 24% Year-Over-Year to $2.49 Million; Sequential Revenue Increased 11% Over Q2 2026.

 

●  Achieved Positive Operating Income: Generated approximately $30,000 of operating income, representing the Company’s first positive quarterly operating result since the third quarter of 2024.

 

●  Gross Profit Increased 19% to $1.47 Million; Gross Margin was 59.0%.

 

●  Operating Expenses Decreased 7% Year-Over-Year and 12% Sequentially, reflecting continued expense discipline.

 

●  Sales Backlog Reached Approximately $2.5 Million at Quarter-End and Approximately $2.8 Million Subsequently, supporting near-term revenue visibility.

 

●  The company expects shareholders’ equity at September 30, 2026 to be in the range of $3 Million to $4 Million

 

The financial results in this press release are preliminary and unaudited. The Company and its independent registered public accounting firm have not yet completed their review of the accounting treatment for the Company’s convertible notes, including the conversion of $2.5 million of notes during the third quarter. Accordingly, this release does not include financial results that may be affected by that review, including any gain or loss associated with the conversion, net loss, net loss per share, and the related liability and stockholders’ equity balances. Based on information currently available, the Company does not expect the review to affect the revenue, gross profit, operating expense or income from operations figures presented in this release; however, all results remain subject to change until the Company files its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the period ended September 30, 2026.

 

Executive Commentary

 

Dr. Halden Shane, CEO of TOMI Environmental Solutions, commented, “The third quarter represented an important inflection point for TOMI. Revenue increased 24% year-over-year and 11% sequentially, and we returned to positive operating income for the first time since the third quarter of 2024. This progress reflects stronger commercial execution, improved equipment sales and disciplined management of our operating cost base.”

 

“We also materially improved our capital structure during the quarter. The conversion of $2.5 million of convertible notes significantly reduced our debt obligations and expected annual cash interest commitment. Combined with an expanding backlog and commercial pipeline, we believe these improvements provide a stronger foundation as we work toward sustained profitability.”

 

“Operationally, momentum continued across mobile equipment, SteraMist applicators, BIT Solution consumables and support services. We also advanced our food safety, life sciences, defense and international regulatory initiatives, including the expanded AgriMist registration and independent Listeria biofilm data. We remain focused on converting our backlog and pipeline, increasing recurring revenue and delivering sustainable growth.”

Q3 2026 Highlights:

 

●  Revenue Growth: Q3 2026 revenue was $2,492,801, a 24% increase from $2,011,556 in Q3 2025 and an 11% sequential increase from $2,246,909 in Q2 2026. Nine-month revenue increased 38% to $6,393,937 from $4,619,229.

 

●  Positive Operating Income: Income from operations was $30,047, compared with an operating loss of ($321,415) in Q3 2025 and an operating loss of ($244,142) in Q2 2026.

 

●  Gross Profit Growth: Gross profit increased 19% to $1,469,921 from $1,231,971 in Q3 2025. Gross margin was 59.0%, compared with 61.2% in the prior-year quarter.

 

●  Disciplined Operating Expenses: Operating expenses decreased 7% year-over-year to $1,439,874 from $1,553,386 and decreased 12% sequentially from $1,629,448 in Q2 2026.

 

●  Product and Domestic Revenue Growth: Product revenue increased 30% to approximately $2.08 million, while U.S. revenue increased 50% to approximately $1.92 million.

 

●  Strong Product and Service Order Growth: Based on 2026 invoiced sales and open orders, iHP Support Services increased approximately 133%, Mobile Equipment increased approximately 106%, and CES/Enclosure sales increased approximately 259% compared with full-year 2025 levels. BIT Solution sales are on track to exceed 2025.

 

●  Expanded Backlog and Pipeline: Sales backlog totaled approximately $2.5 million at September 30, 2026 and subsequently increased to approximately $2.8 million. The Company’s total sales pipeline was approximately $35 million, with approximately 25% in advanced stages.

 

Financial Results for the Three and Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026, Compared to September 30, 2025

 

●  Nine-month performance: Revenue increased 38%, gross profit increased 29%, operating expenses declined 11%, and operating loss improved approximately 62%.

 

●  Sales, net were $2,492,801 for Q3 2026 compared with $2,011,556 for Q3 2025, an increase of $481,245, or 24%. For the nine-month period, sales increased 38% to $6,393,937 from $4,619,229.

 

●  Product revenue increased 30% to approximately $2,078,000 for Q3 2026 from $1,603,000. Service revenue increased 1% to approximately $415,000 from $409,000.

 

●  Geographic performance: U.S. revenue increased 50% to approximately $1,921,000, while international revenue decreased 22% to approximately $572,000.

 

●  Gross profit was $1,469,921, or 59.0% of net sales, compared with $1,231,971, or 61.2% of net sales, for Q3 2025.

 

●  Total operating expenses were $1,439,874, a decrease of $113,512, or 7%, compared with $1,553,386 in Q3 2025.

 

●  Income from operations was $30,047, an improvement of $351,462 compared with a loss from operations of ($321,415) in Q3 2025.

 

Recent Business Highlights:

 

●  On July 20, 2026, the Company effected a 1-for-3 reverse stock split of its Common Stock and Series A Preferred Stock.

 

●  On August 3, 2026, the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency granted a new unconditional registration for SteraMist® (BIT®) – AgriMist (EPA Reg. No. 90150-4), expanding labeled use sites across post-harvest food safety, cannabis and hemp cultivation, and greenhouse and hydroponic agriculture.

 

●  On August 17, 2026, TOMI’s Binary Ionization Technology received formal approval in Ireland under the European Union’s Biocidal Products Regulation, bringing approvals to 11 European markets.

 

●  On September 17, 2026, TOMI reported approximately $2.3 million in SteraMist iHP enclosure purchase orders year-to-date, exceeding approximately $1.6 million in comparable enclosure orders over the prior four years combined.

 

●  On September 18, 2026, TOMI reported year-to-date SteraMist iHP service and support sales of approximately $1.7 million, exceeding the approximately $1.3 million recorded in each of full-year 2024 and full-year 2025.

 

●  On September 21, 2026, TOMI announced the mutual termination of its definitive merger agreement with Carbonium Core, Inc. Each party will bear its own fees and expenses, and no termination fee was payable.

 

●  On September 29, 2026, TOMI announced modular integration concepts for its SteraMist iHP platform targeting autonomous vehicle fleets, drone platforms and robotic systems.

 

●  On September 30, 2026, TOMI announced that Project MEND implemented SteraMist iHP to disinfect reusable durable medical equipment before redistribution.

Looking Ahead

 

TOMI enters the fourth quarter of 2026 with improved operating performance, a strengthened capital structure and growing commercial visibility. The Company is executing a focused strategy to:

 

●  Convert Commercial Pipeline and Backlog: Deliver on the Company’s approximately $2.8 million post-quarter backlog and convert opportunities within its approximately $35 million sales pipeline.

 

●  Drive Recurring Revenue: Expand BIT Solution consumable sales, applicator adoption and iHP service and support revenue across life sciences, healthcare, food safety and commercial markets.

 

●  Advance Food Safety and Regulatory Pathways: Build on the expanded AgriMist EPA registration, independent Listeria biofilm data and ongoing work toward applicable food-contact and international regulatory authorizations.

 

●  Expand International, Defense and Institutional Sales: Leverage the Company’s regulatory approvals, distributor network and defense-sector relationships to broaden adoption of SteraMist iHP technology.

 

●  Maintain Cost and Capital Discipline: Continue managing operating expenses, liquidity and financing resources while pursuing sustainable profitability.

TOMI™ Environmental Solutions, Inc.: Innovating for a safer world®

 

TOMI™ Environmental Solutions, Inc. (NASDAQ: TOMZ) is a global decontamination and infection prevention company, providing environmental solutions for indoor surface disinfection through the manufacturing, sales and licensing of its premier Binary Ionization Technology® (BIT™) platform. Invented under a defense grant in association with the Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA) of the U.S. Department of Defense, BIT™ solution utilizes a low percentage Hydrogen Peroxide as its only active ingredient to produce a fog of ionized Hydrogen Peroxide (iHP™). Represented by the SteraMist® brand of products, iHP™ produces a germ-killing aerosol that works like a visual non-caustic gas.

 

TOMI products are designed to service a broad spectrum of commercial structures, including hospitals and medical facilities, cruise ships, office buildings, hotel and motel rooms, schools, restaurants, meat and produce processing facilities, military barracks, police and fire departments, and athletic facilities. TOMI products and services have also been used in single-family homes and multi-unit residences.

 

For additional information, please visit https://www.steramist.com or contact us at info@tomimist.com.
