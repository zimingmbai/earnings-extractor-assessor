Exhibit 99.1 Earnings Press Release, dated November 12, 2026

 

 

SKYX Reports Record Revenue of $25.7M in Q-3 2026 Compared to $23.9M in Q-3 2025 and 11 Consecutive Quarters of Growth YoY

 

Gross profit rises in Q-3 by 11% to a record $8.1 million as SKYX continues to expand its market penetration

 

Company maintains a strong cash position with $21 million in cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash at September 30, 2026. Management believes it has sufficient cash to support its path to cash-flow positive

 

SKYX Deako merger expands the builder channel pipeline to approximately 4 million units with an expected average selling price of $50 per product

 

Since the merger, SKYX and Deako have announced they expect to supply more than 210,000 units to three prominent builders

 

MIAMI, FL – November 12, 2026 – SKYX Platforms Corp. (NASDAQ: SKYX) (the “Company” or “SKYX”), an award-winning, highly disruptive advanced plug and play smart home and AI platform technology company with over 120 U.S. and global pending and issued patents and with a mission to make homes and buildings advanced, smart and safe as the new standard, today reported financial and operating results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026 and provided business updates.

 

SKYX will host a conference call on November 12, 2026, at 4:30 p.m. Eastern Time to discuss the results and recent developments. Conference call and webcast information appears below.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

 

●Revenue increased 7.4% to $25.7 million from $23.9 million in the third quarter of 2025.
●Gross profit increased 11% to $8.1 million from $7.3 million in the second quarter of 2026.
●Gross margin increased by 9% to 31.4%, compared to 28.9% in the second quarter of 2026.
●$21 million in cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash as of September 30, 2026, compared with $10 million on December 31, 2025.
●A majority of Company’s inventory payments to vendors are paid after the Company receives payments from its customers, continuing to leverage its rapid conversion of its e-commerce sales into cash, advancing it cash position using the “Dell Working Capital Model”, and lowering its cost of capital.
●Net cash used in operating activities decreased by 15.5% to $4.2 million from $5.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. Management believes it has sufficient cash to support its path to cash flow positive.
●Interest-bearing debt, after debt discounts, decreased $3.6 million to $14.7 million from $18.3 million at year-end.
●Adjusted EBITDA loss, a non-GAAP measure, decreased 18.7% to $2.9 million from $3.5 million in the second quarter of 2026. Adjusted EBITDA loss per share, a non-GAAP measure, decreased to $0.02 compared with $0.03 in the second quarter of 2026.

 

 

 

 

Deako Merger and Builder Programs

 

Subsequent to quarter-end, SKYX completed its merger with Deako on October 13, 2026, combining SKYX’s ceiling-based plug-and-play and smart home technologies with Deako’s wall-based plug & play smart home and lighting platform. Deako generated revenues of more than $26 million in 2025, has shipped more than 35 million units over five years, and has provided its solutions to more than 50 U.S. homebuilders. Deako also recently extended supply agreements with D.R. Horton and Stone Martin. Following the merger, SKYX announced the following builder programs:

 

●Maronda Homes: SKYX announced its first major builder program, with more than 80,000 units of their smart home technologies to more than 1,000 homes, including projects in Ohio, Georgia, Maryland and Pennsylvania.
●Home Creations: SKYX announced a second major builder program, with more than 60,000 units expected for more than 700 homes in Oklahoma and Texas.
●Stone Martin: SKYX announced a third home builder program with approximately 70,000 units expected for more than 850 homes in Alabama and Georgia.
●Together, the Home Creations, Maronda and Stone Martin home builder programs are expected to represent more than 210,000 units across more than 2,500 homes. Deliveries are expected through 2027.
●The combined SKYX and Deako pipeline is estimated at approximately 4 million units, with an expected average selling price of approximately $50 per product.
●Management believes the full product portfolio could increase Deako’s revenue per home by up to five times.
●The merger is expected to reduce annual cash expenses by more than $6 million.
●Merger is expected to create future revenue opportunities through product upgrades and interchangeability, AI-enabled services, monitoring, subscriptions and licensing.
●In consideration for the merger, SKYX issued 25,000,000 shares of common stock, subject to a 12-month full lock-up (followed by quarterly leak-out releases at 12, 15, 18, and 21 months after the closing, with share sales subject to a Rule 10b5-1 trading plan).

 

Hospitality and International Expansion

 

●SKYX signed an agreement to supply its technologies to two 65-story luxury Trump Towers in Hyderabad, India, comprising more than 2.2 million square feet. The Company expects to deploy tens of thousands of units in the project.
●SKYX announced plans to supply its technologies during a Marriott city-center hotel renovation in Durham, North Carolina. In Europe, its technology is expected to become a brand standard for developer Group OTT, which has developed more than 250 hotels and buildings. Previously announced hotel projects included the Grand Hotel du Parc in France and the Hotel Mozart Prague, with OTT Heritage Hospitality supporting marketing to the broader European hotel market.
●The Company’s future project opportunities include Miami’s $4 billion Smart City development and projects in North Carolina, Austin, San Antonio, South Florida, New York, Europe, Saudi Arabia and Egypt.

 

 

 

 

Retail Technology and Licensing

 

●Turbo Heater Celling Fans. Company reported that based on growing sales and winter demand for its patented all-in-one turbo heater ceiling fan, it will launch an additional four models in larger and smaller sizes 20” 44” 52” and 60” and in five different colors including white, black, brush nickel, gold, and bronze in addition to its original 24” model across national retailers and its e-commerce platform comprised of multiple websites in the fourth quarter. Retail channel includes The Home Depot, Lowe’s, Target, Walmart, and Amazon.
●Licensing. SKYX has signed a licensing agreement with lighting company Eurofase and plans to pursue additional licensing opportunities. It is also planning an AI-driven system for its e-commerce platform intended to improve conversion rates and sales. The Company has secured manufacturing partnerships in the United States, Vietnam, Taiwan, China and Cambodia.

 

Safety Code Standardization

 

●Company’s code standardization code team continues to pursue mandatory safety-code standardization for its electrical ceiling outlet and receptacle technology. The code team is led by Mark Earley, former head of the National Electrical Code (NEC), and Eric Jacobson, former President and CEO of the American Lighting Association. The Company’s reported progress includes ANSI/NEMA voting to approve its product specification and its technology being voted into 10 segments of the NEC Code Book. Mandatory adoption remains a Company objective.

Company’s Management, Board members, and Senior Advisors include former CEO’s and executives from Fortune 100 companies including Nielsen, Microsoft, Disney, GE, The Home Depot, Office Depot, Chrysler, among others.

 

In its 11 consecutive YoY comparable quarter growth, the Company continued to increase its revenue in the third quarter of 2026, generating $25.7 million, an increase of 7.4% compared with $23.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. We continue to expand our channel strategy across the builder, professional, commercial, hospitality and retail markets.

Deako’s established relationships with more than 50 U.S. homebuilders provide an additional sales channel for SKYX’s ceiling-based technologies alongside Deako’s plug & play wall-switch and intelligent lighting platform. About SKYX Platforms Corp. (NASDAQ: SKYX)

 

SKYX Platforms Corp. (NASDAQ: SKYX) is an advanced smart home, artificial intelligence (AI), and plug-and-play technology platform company with a mission to make homes and buildings safe, advanced, and smart as the new standard. Following its merger with Deako, Inc., SKYX combines complementary technologies designed to simplify the installation, integration, and upgrading of smart home and building systems across residential, commercial, builder, and hospitality markets.

 

The merged company offers a broad portfolio of plug-and-play platform technologies and products spanning ceiling and wall electrical infrastructure, intelligent lighting and switches, smart home and AI-enabled solutions, and other connected home and building applications. By bringing together SKYX’s ceiling-based technologies and Deako’s established wall-switch platform, the company enables a more comprehensive approach to smart homes and buildings, reducing the complexity associated with traditional wired installations while enhancing convenience, safety, and functionality.

 

SKYX has a combined portfolio of more than 120 issued and pending in the U.S. and internationally patents and patent applications relating to smart home and building platforms, AI, and plug-and-play technologies. Including its wholly owned subsidiary, Deako, SKYX has supplied and expects to supply plug & play smart lighting technologies to more than 60 U.S. homebuilders and builders. The merger expands SKYX’s opportunities across the homebuilder, professional, commercial, and hospitality markets, while creating potential for future recurring revenue through product upgrades and interchangeability, AI-enabled services, monitoring, subscriptions, and licensing.

 

In addition to its technology platforms, SKYX owns an e-commerce platform comprised of 60 e-commerce lighting and home décor websites serving retail and commercial customers. Through its combined technologies and product portfolio, SKYX is focused on advancing the adoption of smart, connected, and safer environments in homes and buildings across the United States and internationally.

 

For more information, please visit www.skyx.com and www.deako.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

Management considers earnings (loss) before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, or EBITDA, as adjusted, an important indicator in evaluating our business on a consistent basis across various periods. Due to the significance of non-recurring items, EBITDA, as adjusted, enables our management to monitor and evaluate our business on a consistent basis. We use EBITDA, as adjusted, as a primary measure, among others, to analyze and evaluate financial and strategic planning decisions regarding future operating investments and potential acquisitions. We believe that EBITDA, as adjusted, eliminates items that are not part of our core operations, such as interest expense and amortization and impairment expense associated with intangible assets, or items that do not involve a cash outlay, such as share-based payments and non-recurring items, such as transaction costs. EBITDA, as adjusted, should be considered in addition to, rather than as a substitute for, pre-tax income (loss), net income (loss) and cash flows used in operating activities. This non-GAAP financial measure excludes significant expenses that are required by GAAP to be recorded in our financial statements and is subject to inherent limitations. Investors should review the reconciliation of this non-GAAP financial measure to the comparable GAAP financial measure included below. Investors should not rely on any single financial measure to evaluate our business.
