Discussion of financial results for the three months ended September 30, 2026 (all comparisons are to third quarter 2025, unless otherwise noted):

·

Net income was $11.8 million, an increase of $3.4 million from $8.3 million. Diluted earnings per share were $1.08 compared to $0.89.

·

Net interest income was $28.0 million, an increase of $7.6 million from $20.5 million. On a linked-quarter basis, net interest income increased $1.2 million from $26.8 million.

·

Net interest margin (fully taxable equivalent) was 4.01% compared to 3.63%. On a linked-quarter basis, NIM increased 11 basis points from 3.90%.

·

Pre-provision net revenue (PPNR) was $15.4 million, compared to $10.0 million in the prior year quarter and $13.6 million in the linked quarter.

·

Total assets were $2.951 billion, compared to $2.412 billion, an increase of 18.3%.

·

Loans receivable were $2.294 billion compared to $1.815 billion, an increase of 20.9%.

·

Total deposits were $2.509 billion compared to $2.074 billion, an increase of 17.3%.

·

Tangible book value per share was $22.35 compared to $21.19 at September 30, 2025, and decreased $0.61 on a linked-quarter basis from $22.96.

·

Allowance for credit losses to total loans was 1.10%. Nonperforming loans to total loans were 0.73% and nonperforming assets to total assets were 0.59%.

Follow-up on Second Quarter Credit Event

During the second quarter of 2026, the Company recorded a $729 thousand charge-off related to a borrower relationship that filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection.

During the third quarter, one of the loans that had been placed on non-accrual, due to the bankruptcy previously disclosed, was purchased by a new entity, as was the underlying business. All past due interest from the previous loan was paid. During the period that the business was in bankruptcy, Wayne Bank advanced debtor-in-possession (DIP) financing to keep the business operating. The DIP financing along with all interest and fees was settled as part of the loan purchase. Accordingly, interest income increased in the third quarter which, in turn, increased our margin during the quarter. Additionally, removing the previous loan from non-accrual status resulted in a lower provision expense for the quarter. 

Following the purchase of this loan, there remain two loans related to the borrower with a total exposure of $7.1 million.
