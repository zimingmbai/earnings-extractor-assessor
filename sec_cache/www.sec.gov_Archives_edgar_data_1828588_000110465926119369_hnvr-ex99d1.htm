Results Highlighted by Increased Net Interest

Declares $0.10 Quarterly Cash Dividend

​

Third Quarter Performance Highlights

​

●Net Income: Net income for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 totaled $4.3 million or $0.60 per diluted share (including Series A preferred shares), versus $4.1 million or $0.55 per diluted share (including Series A preferred shares) in the quarter ended June 30, 2026 and $3.5 million or $0.47 per diluted share (including Series A preferred shares) in the quarter ended September 30, 2025.
●Pre-Provision Net Revenue: Pre-provision net revenue (non-GAAP) was $6.9 million or 1.18% of average assets for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, which was the highest level since the fourth quarter of 2022, versus $5.9 million or 1.06% of average assets in the prior linked quarter and $6.0 million or 1.05% of average assets in the third quarter of 2025.
●Balance Sheet Growth:  Total assets were $2.4 billion at September 30, 2026, up $105.2 million, or 4.50%, from June 30, 2026 and up $58.7 million, or 2.46% from December 31, 2025 due to strong loan growth.
●Strong Loan Growth: Total loans outstanding were $2.1 billion at September 30, 2026, up $86.8 million, or 4.34%, from June 30, 2026 and up $83.9 million, or 4.19% from December 31, 2025, primarily driven by growth in niche-residential and relationship CRE and C&I lending.  Loan production for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $178.7 million, up 69.70% from the linked quarter.
●Net Interest Income: Net interest income was $17.0 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, an increase of $0.3 million, or 1.52% from the quarter ended June 30, 2026 and $1.8 million, or 11.84%, from the quarter ended September 30, 2025, representing a record level.
●Improved Profitability Metrics: Returns on average assets and average tangible equity increased to 0.75% and 9.32%, respectively, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 from 0.73% and 8.85%, respectively, for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 and 0.61% and 7.63%, respectively, for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.  Efficiency ratio improved to 64.3% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, versus 69.8% from the quarter ended June 30, 2026 and  66.7% from the quarter ended September 30, 2025.  The efficiency ratio improvement on a linked quarter basis is primarily related to a $1.3 million, or 9.18% decrease in non-interest expense, and from the quarter ended September 30, 2025, a $1.8 million or 11.84% increase in net interest income.
●Demand Deposits: Demand deposits increased $7.3 million or 2.96% to a record $255.1 million at September 30, 2026 from December 31, 2025, driven by the success of our C&I banking relationships and an increase in municipal operating accounts.
●Book Value Per Share: Book value per share (including Series A preferred shares) was $27.95 at September 30, 2026 compared to $27.66 at June 30, 2026 and $27.02 at December 31, 2025.  Tangible book value per share was $25.29 at September 30, 2026 compared to $25.02 at June 30, 2026 and $24.41 at December 31, 2025.  Exclusive of Accumulated Other Comprehensive Loss, book value per share and tangible book value per share would have increased by $0.32 and $0.31, respectively, at September 30, 2026.  Tangible common equity to tangible assets (“TCE ratio”) was 7.58% at September 30, 2026 compared to 7.91% at June 30, 2026 and 7.65% at December 31, 2025.  The decrease in TCE ratio over the linked quarter is primarily related to a $1.8 million increase in after tax net unrealized loss on the investment portfolio and $2.2 million in common stock repurchases.
●Quarterly Cash Dividend: The Company’s Board of Directors approved a $0.10 per share cash dividend on both common shares and Series A preferred shares payable on November 18, 2026 to stockholders of record on November 11, 2026.
●Business Banking Center Update: Our newest de novo branches in Hauppauge and Port Jefferson continue to demonstrate robust growth.  As of September 30, 2026, the Hauppauge branch achieved $149.1 million in deposits with a 1.64% cost of funds, while the Port Jefferson location reached $52.1 million in deposits with a 1.85% cost of funds.  Building on this momentum, our new full-service branch in downtown Riverhead, New York officially opened on September 30, 2026.  This strategic expansion enables us to better serve the East End of Long Island with our complete suite of financial and banking services.

​

Mineola, NY – October 28, 2026 – Hanover Bancorp, Inc. (“Hanover” or “the Company” – NASDAQ: HNVR), the holding company for Hanover Community Bank (“the Bank”), today reported results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 and the declaration of a $0.10 per share cash dividend on both common shares and Series A preferred shares payable on November 18, 2026 to stockholders of record on November 11, 2026.

​

Earnings Summary for the Quarter Ended September 30, 2026

​

The Company reported net income for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 of $4.3 million or $0.60 per diluted share (including Series A preferred shares) versus $3.5 million or $0.47 per diluted share (including Series A preferred shares) for the quarter ended September 30, 2025 and $4.1 million or $0.55 per diluted share (including Series A preferred shares) for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.  Returns on average assets, average stockholders’ equity and average tangible equity were 0.75%, 8.44% and 9.32%, respectively, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, versus 0.61%, 6.90% and 7.63%, respectively, for the comparable quarter of 2025, and 0.73%, 8.01% and 8.85%, respectively, for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

​

The increase in net income recorded in the third quarter of 2026 from the comparable 2025 quarter resulted from an increase in net interest income and a decrease in provision for credit losses.  This was partially offset by a decrease in non-interest income, consisting primarily of a decrease in gain on sale of loans held-for-sale due to a lower volume of SBA loan sales, an increase in non-interest expense, and an increase in income tax expense.

​

The increase in net income recorded over the prior linked quarter resulted from an increase in net interest income and a decrease in non-interest expense.  This was partially offset by a decrease in non-interest income, consisting primarily of a decrease in gain on sale of loans held-for-sale and loan servicing and fee income, and increases in the provision for credit losses and income tax expense.  The decrease in non-interest expense is primarily related to decreases in compensation and benefits, professional fees (particularly legal), and other operating expenses, primarily SBA broker fees.  In the June  30, 2026 quarter, the Company incurred $240 thousand of debt extinguishment charges which were recorded in other operating expenses.

​

Net interest income was $17.0 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, an increase of $1.8 million, or 11.84% from the comparable 2025 quarter.  This increase was due to improvement in the Company’s net interest margin to 3.02% in the 2026 quarter from 2.74% in the comparable 2025 quarter.  The cost of interest-bearing liabilities decreased to 3.50% in the 2026 quarter from 3.89% in the comparable 2025 quarter, a decrease of 39 basis points.  This decrease was partially offset by a 5 basis point decrease in the yield on interest earning assets to 5.89% in the 2026 quarter from 5.94% in the third quarter of 2025.  Net interest income on a linked quarter basis increased by $0.3 million or 1.52%.

Earnings Summary for the Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026

​

For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company reported net income of $10.3 million or $1.40 per diluted share (including Series A preferred shares), versus $7.5 million or $1.00 per diluted share (including Series A preferred shares) in the comparable 2025 nine-month period.  The Company recorded adjusted (non-GAAP) net income (excluding debt extinguishment charges and severance expenses of $2.3 million, net of tax) of $12.6 million or $1.71 per diluted share in the nine months ended September 30, 2026, versus adjusted (non-GAAP) net income (excluding core system conversion expenses of $2.6 million, net of tax) of $10.0 million or $1.34 per diluted share in the comparable 2025 nine-month period.  Returns on average assets, average stockholders’ equity and average tangible equity were 0.60%, 6.75% and 7.46%, respectively, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, versus 0.44%, 5.00% and 5.54%, respectively, for the comparable 2025 period.  Adjusted (non-GAAP) returns, exclusive of debt extinguishment charges and severance expenses, on average assets, average stockholders’ equity and average tangible equity were 0.74%, 8.28% and 9.15%, respectively, in the nine months ended September 30, 2026, versus 0.60%, 6.72% and 7.45%, respectively, in the comparable 2025 period, exclusive of core system conversion expenses.

​

The increase in net income recorded for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 from the comparable 2025 period is due to an increase in net interest income and a decrease in the provision for credit losses.  These were partially offset by a decrease in non-interest income, consisting primarily of a decrease in gain on sale of loans held-for-sale, an increase in income tax expense and an increase in non-interest expenses, particularly compensation and benefits, severance expenses, occupancy and equipment and other operating expenses which included $240 thousand of debt extinguishment charges.  In February 2025, the Company incurred $3.2 million of one-time core system conversion expenses.  Severance expenses for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 were primarily related to a severance benefit of approximately $2.2 million to the former President of the Company and the Bank whose last day of employment was March 31, 2026.

​

Net interest income was $50.2 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of $5.5 million, or 12.35% from the comparable 2025 period, due to the improvement of the Company’s net interest margin to 3.03% in the 2026 period from 2.73% in the comparable 2025 period.  The cost of interest-bearing liabilities decreased to 3.49% in the nine months ended September 30, 2026 from 3.95% in the comparable 2025 period, a decrease of 46 basis points.  This decrease was partially offset by a 9 basis point decrease in the yield on interest earning assets to 5.89% in the 2026 period from 5.98% in the comparable 2025 period.

Balance Sheet Highlights

​

Total assets were $2.44 billion at September 30, 2026 versus $2.38 billion at December 31, 2025.  Total securities available for sale (“AFS”) at September 30, 2026 were $138.2 million, an increase of $38.7 million from December 31, 2025, primarily driven by growth in U.S. GSE residential mortgage-backed securities, collateralized loan obligations and corporate bonds, offset by decreases in U.S. Treasury securities and collateralized mortgage obligations.

​

Total deposits were $2.11 billion at September 30, 2026 versus $2.03 billion at December 31, 2025.  Our loan to deposit ratio was 99% both at September 30, 2026 and December 31, 2025.

​

3

Borrowings at September 30, 2026 were $59.8 million, with a weighted average rate and term of 3.49% and 48 months, respectively.  At September 30, 2026 and December 31, 2025, the Company had $59.8 million (net of $410 thousand deferred prepayment penalty) and $100.7 million, respectively, of term FHLB advances outstanding.  The Company had no FHLB overnight borrowings outstanding at September 30, 2026 and December 31, 2025.  The Company had no borrowings outstanding under lines of credit with correspondent banks at September 30, 2026 and December 31, 2025.

​

Stockholders’ equity was $202.9 million at September 30, 2026 as compared to $200.3 million at December 31, 2025.  Retained earnings increased by $8.1 million due primarily to net income of $10.3 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, which was offset by $2.2 million of dividends declared.  The accumulated other comprehensive loss at September 30, 2026 was 1.13% of total equity and was comprised of a $2.3 million after tax net unrealized loss on the investment portfolio that was partially offset by a $0.1 million after tax net unrealized gain on derivatives.  During the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company repurchased 192,718 shares of its common stock at an aggregate cost of $4.8 million.  Book value per share (including Series A preferred shares) increased to $27.95 at September 30, 2026 from $27.02 at December 31, 2025.  Tangible book value per share (including Series A preferred shares) increased to $25.29 at September 30, 2026 from $24.41 at December 31, 2025.

Loan Portfolio

​

The Bank’s loan portfolio was $2.08 billion at September 30, 2026 and $2.0 billion at December 31, 2025.  At September 30, 2026, the Company’s residential loan portfolio (including home equity) amounted to $806.5 million, with an average loan balance of $494 thousand and a weighted average loan-to-value ratio of 56%.  Commercial real estate (including construction) and multifamily loans totaled $1.12 billion at September 30, 2026, with an average loan balance of $1.6 million and a weighted average loan-to-value ratio of 58%.  As discussed below, approximately 34% of the multifamily portfolio is subject to rent regulation.  The Company’s commercial real estate concentration ratio decreased to 358% of capital at September 30, 2026 from 362% at December 31, 2025, with loans secured by office space accounting for 2% of the total loan portfolio and totaling $41.2 million at September 30, 2026.  The Company’s loan pipeline at September 30, 2026 is approximately $221.1 million.

​

The Bank originates loans for its portfolio and for sale in the secondary market under a residential flow origination program.  During the quarters ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, the Company sold $18.7 million and $21.4 million, respectively, of residential loans under its flow origination program and recorded gains on sale of loans held-for-sale of $0.5 million and $0.5 million, respectively.  Residential loan originations were $110 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

​

During the quarters ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, the Company sold approximately $1.3 million and $11.4 million, respectively, of government guaranteed SBA loans and recorded gains on sale of loans held-for-sale of $0.1 million and $0.9 million, respectively.  SBA loan originations and gains on sale continue to be lower due to a less favorable economic outlook for many business owners along with the Bank’s ongoing prudent decision to tighten credit. Together, these factors contributed to lower SBA loan volume, approval levels, and related gain-on-sale income.

Office Property Exposure

​

The Bank’s exposure to the Office market is not significant.  Loans secured by office space accounted for 2% of the total loan portfolio at September 30, 2026, with a total balance of $41.2 million, of which less than 1% is located in Manhattan.  The pool has a 2.94x weighted average DSCR and a 55% weighted average LTV as of September 30, 2026.

​

Asset Quality and Allowance for Credit Losses

​

At September 30, 2026, the Bank reported $29.1 million in non-performing loans compared to $28.3 million at June 30, 2026 and $21.6 million at December 31, 2025.  Excluding the portion that is government guaranteed by the SBA, non-performing loans were $22.1 million at September 30, 2026 compared to $21.3 million at June 30, 2026 and $17.9 million at December 31, 2025.  At September 30, 2026 non-performing loans were 1.40% of total loans outstanding versus 1.08% at December 31, 2025.  Excluding the SBA guaranteed portion, non-performing loans were 1.06% of total loans outstanding at September 30, 2026 versus 0.90% at December 31, 2025.

​

During the third quarter of 2026, the Bank recorded a provision for credit losses of $1.1 million.  The allowance for credit losses was $19.9 million at September 30, 2026 versus $18.7 million at December 31, 2025.  The allowance for credit losses as a percentage of total loans was 0.96% at September 30, 2026 and 0.93% at December 31, 2025.

​

Net Interest Margin

​

The Bank’s net interest margin increased to 3.02% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 compared to 2.74% in the quarter ended September 30, 2025.  The Bank’s net interest margin was 3.10% in the quarter ended June 30, 2026.  The decrease from the prior linked quarter was primarily related to an increase in the average cost of deposits due to the anticipated Federal Reserve interest rate hike and the competitive deposit environment. Any further increases in cost of funds are expected to be mitigated by higher yielding loan production and rate resets.
