MAYVILLE ENGINEERING COMPANY ANNOUNCES

THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS

MILWAUKEE, Wis., November 3, 2026 – Mayville Engineering Company (NYSE: MEC) (the “Company” or “MEC”), a leading value-added provider of design, prototyping and manufacturing solutions serving diverse end markets, today announced results for the three months ended September 30, 2026.

THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS

(All comparisons versus the prior-year period)

​

●Net sales of $170.4 million, or +18.1% y/y
●Net income of $0.4 million, or $0.02 per diluted share; Non-GAAP Adjusted Diluted EPS of $0.15
●Adjusted EBITDA of $17.0 million
●Adjusted EBITDA margin of 10.0% of net sales
●Ratio of net debt to trailing twelve-month Adjusted EBITDA of 2.9x1 as of September 30, 2026
●Secured approximately $45 million in Datacenter & Critical Power project awards

1 Trailing twelve-month Adjusted EBITDA is calculated per the Company’s current Credit Agreement

MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY

“Our third quarter results were in line with our expectations and reflected strong demand as project volumes continued to accelerate within our Datacenter & Critical Power end market,” said Jag Reddy, President and Chief Executive Officer. “Net sales increased 18.1% year-over-year, driven by continued growth in Datacenter & Critical Power and improving demand across our Commercial Vehicle and Construction & Access end markets. As volumes ramped, Adjusted EBITDA margin improved on a sequential basis through better capacity utilization, while we continued to absorb project launch costs consistent with our expectations. At the same time, operational efficiency across our footprint remained uneven during the quarter as our teams responded to rapidly evolving customer requirements and the pace and volume of incoming demand. We view these dynamics as a natural part of scaling the business through a period of accelerated growth, and our priority remains investing in the people, equipment and capacity required to capture what we believe is a historic, multi-year growth opportunity in the high-value Datacenter & Critical Power space.”

“During the quarter, we secured approximately $45 million of new Datacenter & Critical Power project awards, and our qualified opportunity pipeline now exceeds $200 million,” Reddy continued. “Importantly, this demand we are seeing today reflects datacenter infrastructure investments initiated approximately three years ago, as the products we manufacture are typically installed during the later stages of a project build. Based on our awarded programs and pipeline, we are anticipating reaching full capacity within the next 18 months and have started deploying capital towards expanding our footprint. On October 1, 2026 we closed on the acquisition of our Thomson, Georgia facility, which, together with planned equipment investment, is expected to add approximately $50 million of annual revenue capacity by 2028 and increase our total revenue capacity beyond the approximately $850 million previously disclosed.”

​

“Looking ahead, I am excited about the opportunity in front of us and our ability to deliver profitable growth in 2027 and beyond,” Reddy concluded. “We will continue to incur launch costs and deploy capital in a disciplined manner as we position MEC to serve this growing customer base. While these investments and the ongoing alignment of our operations with customer timelines will remain a factor over the coming quarters, we expect to realize improved operating leverage as volumes continue to ramp. We believe these actions position MEC to deliver attractive, sustainable returns on invested capital as we capitalize on this multi-year secular growth opportunity.”

PERFORMANCE SUMMARY

Net sales increased by 18.1% on a year-over-year basis in the third quarter of 2026, primarily due to growing demand in the Datacenter & Critical Power, Commercial Vehicle, Construction & Access and Other end markets. These increased volumes were partially offset by lower demand in the Powersports and Agriculture end markets.

Manufacturing margin was $18.8 million in the third quarter of 2026, or 11.0% of net sales, versus $15.9 million, or 11.0% of net sales, in the prior year period. The year-over-year increase in manufacturing margin dollars was primarily attributable to improved capacity utilization driven by higher sales volumes in the Datacenter & Critical Power end market, as well as growth within the Commercial Vehicle and Construction & Access end markets. This increase was partially offset by $3.0 million of Datacenter & Critical Power project launch and outsourcing costs, and premium labor.

Bonuses and deferred compensation expenses were $2.5 million in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $2.2 million in the prior year period. Other selling, general and administrative expenses were $8.8 million in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $10.5 million in the prior year period. The decrease in other selling, general and administrative expenses primarily reflect a decrease in non-recurring acquisition-related expenses and the realization of synergies associated with the Accu-Fab acquisition.

Interest expense was $3.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $3.4 million in the prior year period, due to decreased average borrowings under the Company’s revolving credit facility partially offset by higher interest rates in the current period.

Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $0.4 million, or $0.02 per diluted share, versus a net loss of $2.7 million, or ($0.13) per diluted share, in the prior year period.

MEC reported Adjusted EBITDA of $17.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, or 10.0% of net sales, versus $14.1 million, or 9.8% of net sales, in the prior year period. The increase in Adjusted EBITDA is primarily due to higher sales volumes and improved capacity utilization, partially offset by Datacenter & Critical Power project launch and outsourcing costs, and premium labor.

Third quarter Adjusted Net Income was $4.0 million, or $0.15 per diluted share, versus $2.0 million, or $0.10 per diluted share, in the prior year period. The increase in Adjusted Net Income reflects higher income from operations and lower interest expense.

Free cash flow during the third quarter of 2026 was ($3.3) million as compared to ($1.1) million in the prior year period. The decrease was primarily driven by higher capital expenditures related to equipment needed to support rapidly accelerating demand in the Datacenter & Critical Power end market partially offset by higher cash flow from operations.

Commercial Vehicle

MEC is a Tier 1 supplier to many of the country’s top original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) of commercial vehicles providing exhaust & aftertreatment, engine components, cooling, fuel and structural systems for both heavy- and medium-duty commercial vehicles.

Net sales to the Commercial Vehicle end market were $52.8 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 34.7% versus the prior year period. The increase was attributable to improved customer demand during the third quarter of 2026, supported by an increase in North American Class 8 production.

Datacenter & Critical Power

MEC manufactures precision metal enclosures, racks, frames, and sub-assemblies for OEMs that deliver reliable power distribution, backup energy systems, and intelligent power management solutions in mission-critical datacenter and electrical infrastructure environments.

Net sales to the Datacenter & Critical Power end market were $37.2 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 64.9% versus the prior year period. The increase in sales reflects accelerating demand from legacy customers and new project launches.

Construction & Access

MEC manufactures components and sub-assemblies for OEMs within the construction & access market including fenders, hoods, supports, frames, platforms, frame structures, doors and tubular products such as exhaust & aftertreatment, engine components, cooling system components, handrails and full electro-mechanical assemblies.

Net sales to the Construction & Access end market were $24.2 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 9.3% versus the prior year period. The increase in net sales was primarily due to continued improvement in non-residential construction demand.

Powersports

MEC manufactures stampings and complex metal assemblies and coatings for OEMs within the all-terrain vehicles (ATV), side-by-sides, utility task vehicles (UTV), marine propulsion, and motorcycle markets. MEC’s powersports expertise includes axle housings, steering columns, swing arms, fenders, suspension components, ATV/UTV racks, cowl assemblies and vehicle frames.

​

Net sales to the Powersports end market were $16.8 million in the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of 26.9% versus the prior year period. The decrease in net sales to the Powersports end market was primarily driven by continued soft customer demand due to global supply chain realignment among OEMs in this end market.

Agriculture

MEC is an integral partner in the supply chain of the world’s leading agriculture OEMs manufacturing components and sub-assemblies including fenders, hoods, supports, frames, platforms, frame structures, doors, and tubular products such as exhaust, engine components, cooling system components, handrails and full electro-mechanical assemblies.

Net sales to the Agriculture end market were $7.3 million in the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of 9.9% versus the prior year period. The decrease in net sales in the Agriculture end market reflects continued demand softness for large agricultural equipment.

Military

MEC holds the International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR) certification and produces components for the United States military. Products include exhaust, engine components, cooling, fuel, suspension, structural systems, and chemical agent resistant coating (CARC) painting capabilities.

Net sales to the Military end market were $7.2 million in the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of 2.8% versus the prior year period. The decrease in net sales compared to the prior year was due to continued program transition delays.

Other

MEC also produces a wide variety of components and assemblies for customers in the industrial equipment & fixtures, consumer tools, mining, forestry, automotive, and medical markets.

Net sales to Other end markets for the third quarter of 2026 were $24.8 million, an increase of 13.5% versus the prior year period. The increase in net sales compared to the prior year period was associated with continued strong aluminum extrusion demand.

BALANCE SHEET UPDATE

As of September 30, 2026, MEC had net debt outstanding of $146.6 million and total cash and availability on its senior secured revolving credit facility of $30.3 million. At the end of the third quarter, the ratio of net debt to trailing twelve-month Adjusted EBITDA was 2.9x.

ABOUT MAYVILLE ENGINEERING COMPANY

​

Founded in 1945, MEC is a leading U.S.-based, vertically-integrated, value-added manufacturing partner providing a full suite of manufacturing solutions from concept to production, including design, prototyping and tooling, fabrication, aluminum extrusion, coating, assembly and aftermarket components. Our customers operate in diverse end markets, including heavy- and medium-duty commercial vehicles, datacenter & critical power, construction & access equipment, powersports, agriculture, military and other end markets. Along with process engineering and development services, MEC maintains an extensive manufacturing infrastructure with 28 facilities, of which 25 are in use, across ten states. These facilities make it possible to offer conventional and CNC (computer numerical control) stamping, shearing, fiber laser cutting, forming, drilling, tapping, grinding, tube bending, machining, welding, assembly, and logistics services. MEC also possesses a broad range of finishing capabilities including shot blasting, e-coating, powder coating, wet spray and military grade chemical agent resistant coating (CARC) painting. For more information, please visit www.mecinc.com.

NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES

This press release contains financial information calculated in a manner other than in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”).

​

The non-GAAP measures used in this press release are EBITDA, EBITDA Margin, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Net Income (Loss), Adjusted Diluted EPS and Free Cash Flow.

​

EBITDA represents net income (loss) before interest expense, provision (benefit) for income taxes, depreciation, and amortization. EBITDA Margin represents EBITDA as a percentage of net sales for each period. Adjusted EBITDA represents EBITDA before stock-based compensation expense, loss on extinguishment of debt, Chief Financial Officer transition costs, natural disaster costs, acquisition related costs, costs recognized on step-up of Accu-Fab acquired inventory, restructuring and impairment costs. Adjusted EBITDA Margin represents Adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of net sales for each period. Adjusted Net Income (Loss) and Adjusted Diluted EPS represent net income (loss) before the aforementioned Adjusted EBITDA addback items and acquisition related amortization of intangible assets, which do not reflect our core operating performance. Free Cash Flow represents net cash provided by, or used in, operating activities, less cash flows used in the purchase of property, plant and equipment. We present Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Net Income (Loss), Adjusted Diluted EPS and Free Cash Flow as management uses these measures as key performance indicators, and we believe they are measures frequently used by securities analysts, investors and other parties to evaluate companies in our industry. These metrics are supplemental measures of our operating performance that are neither required by, nor presented in accordance with, GAAP. These measures should not be considered as an alternative to net income (loss) or cash flow provided by, or used in, operating activities, or any other performance measure derived in accordance with GAAP as an indicator of our operating performance. These measures may not be comparable to the similarly named measures reported by other companies and have limitations as analytical tools and should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for analysis of our results as reported under GAAP.

​

Please reference our reconciliation of net income (loss), the most directly comparable measure calculated in accordance with GAAP, to EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Net Income (Loss), Adjusted Diluted EPS, Free Cash Flow and the calculation of EBITDA Margin and Adjusted EBITDA Margin included in this press release.
