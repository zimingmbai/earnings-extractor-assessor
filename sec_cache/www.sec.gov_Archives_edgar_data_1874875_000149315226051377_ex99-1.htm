Hour Loop Reports Results for Three and Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026

 

Continued Profitability Despite a Challenging Economic Environment

 

Redmond, WA, Nov 12, 2026 – Hour Loop, Inc. (NASDAQ: HOUR) (“Hour Loop” or the “Company”), a leading online retailer, announces its financial and operational results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Financial Highlights for Three and Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026:

 

●Net revenues for the third quarter of 2026 decreased 4% to $32.1 million, compared to $33.4 million in the year-ago period;
●Net income for the third quarter of 2026 decreased to $0.4 million, compared to $0.5 million in the year-ago period;
●For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, net revenues increased 11% to $95.9 million, compared to $86.4 million in the year-ago period;
●Net income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 decreased to $2.3 million, compared to $2.4 million for the year-ago period; and
●Cash used in operating activities for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $4.1 million, compared to $0.4 million in the year-ago period.

 

Management Commentary

 

“Through the first nine months of 2026, Hour Loop grew net revenues 11% and earned $2.3 million in net income, and we stayed profitable through a tougher third quarter,” said Sam Lai, Chief Executive Officer and Interim Chief Financial Officer. “We made a deliberate choice to protect our margins rather than chase low-quality sales, and we believe that discipline puts us in a strong position as we head into the holiday season, the most important time of our year.”

 

“We are building an AI-first company, and AI is already changing how we work every day. AI agents now support core parts of our operations – purchasing and replenishment, advertising, pricing, inbound shipments, inventory reconciliation, customer service and compliance. In purchasing, our agents help us make smarter, faster buying decisions. Agents now handle much of the day-to-day work. As routine work shifts to AI, our teams are freed to focus on higher-value decisions, and we believe this will allow us to scale the business without growing headcount at the same pace.”

 

“We are very excited about where Hour Loop is headed. With a disciplined operating model, powered by AI to drive greater efficiency, we are confident in the long-term opportunity ahead. We thank our employees for their drive and our shareholders for their continued support.”

 

Third Quarter of 2026 Financial Results

 

Net revenues in the third quarter of 2026 were $32.1 million, compared to $33.4 million in the same period last year. The decrease was primarily due to lower order volume with lower inventory levels.

 

Gross profit as a percentage of net revenues decreased to 50.6%, compared to 51.4% in the same period last year. The decrease was primarily due to lower inventory reimbursements from platforms, partially offset by tariff-related refunds from vendors.

 

Operating expenses as a percentage of net revenues in the third quarter of 2026 decreased to 49.1%, compared to 49.2% in the same period last year. The decrease was mainly attributable to lower selling and marketing expenses in line with lower revenues.

 

 

 

 

 

Net income in the third quarter of 2026 was $0.4 million, or $0.01 per diluted share, compared to net income of $0.5 million, or $0.02 per diluted share, in the same period last year. The decrease was primarily driven by lower net revenues and gross margin for the reasons mentioned above.

 

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $1.1 million in cash and cash equivalents, compared to $3.8 million as of December 31, 2025. The decrease was primarily driven by investment in inventory ahead of the holiday season.

 

Full Year 2026 Financial Outlook

 

Hour Loop narrows its guidance for the full year, anticipating 2026 net revenue to be in the range of $147.0 million to $152.0 million, representing 3% to 6% year-over-year growth. The Company continues to expect 2026 net income to be in the range of $0.75 million to $1.5 million.

 

About Hour Loop, Inc.

 

Hour Loop is an online retailer engaged in e-commerce retailing in the U.S. market. It has operated as a third-party seller on www.amazon.com and has sold merchandise on its website at www.hourloop.com since 2013. Hour Loop further expanded its operations to other marketplaces such as Walmart, eBay, and Etsy. To date, Hour Loop has generated practically all its revenue as a third-party seller on www.amazon.com and only a negligible amount of revenue from its own website and other marketplaces. Hour Loop manages more than 100,000 stock-keeping units (“SKUs”). Product categories include home/garden décor, toys, kitchenware, apparel, and electronics. Hour Loop’s primary strategy is to bring most of its vendors’ product selections to the customers. It has advanced software that assists Hour Loop in identifying product gaps so it can keep such products in stock year-round, including the entirety of the last quarter (holiday season) of the calendar year. In upcoming years, Hour Loop plans to expand its business rapidly by increasing the number of business managers, vendors, and SKUs.
