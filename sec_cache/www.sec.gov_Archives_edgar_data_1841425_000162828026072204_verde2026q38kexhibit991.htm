Verde Clean Fuels, Inc. Reports Q3 2026 Results
•Maintained strong balance sheet with $52.6 million cash and no debt
•Continuing to evaluate strategic alternatives and opportunities to deploy our STG+® technology
•Continuing to execute on cost savings initiatives

HOUSTON – November 9, 2026 - Verde Clean Fuels, Inc. (“Verde” or the "Company”) (Nasdaq: VGAS) announced today financial results for the third quarter and nine months of 2026.
“We are continuing to evaluate strategic alternatives and opportunities to deploy our STG+® technology with a disciplined approach to capital allocation. We remain focused on preserving shareholder capital and evaluating opportunities to maximize shareholder value," said George Burdette, CEO of Verde.
As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $52.6 million of cash and cash equivalents and no debt. Shares outstanding remained unchanged at 44.5 million shares including both Class A and Class C common stock.
For the third quarter 2026, the Company recorded a net loss of $(2.0) million and diluted net loss per share of Class A common stock of $(0.04) compared to a net loss of $(2.3) million and diluted net loss per share of Class A common stock of $(0.06) for the same period in 2025. For the nine months of 2026, the Company recorded a net loss of $(6.3) million and diluted net loss per share of Class A common stock of $(0.14), compared to a net loss of $(7.6) million and diluted net loss per share of Class A common stock of $(0.21) for the same period in 2025. The lower losses were primarily due to implementation of our cost savings initiatives, which was partially offset by higher share-based compensation expense.
About Verde Clean Fuels, Inc.
Verde owns an innovative and proprietary gas-to-liquids processing technology capable of converting low-value or stranded feedstocks into higher-value clean transportation fuels. Our synthesis gas (“syngas”)-to-gasoline plus (STG+®) process is designed to convert syngas, derived from a variety of feedstocks, including natural gas and biomass, into fully finished liquid fuels that require no additional refining. The STG+® technology is engineered for industrial-scale deployment and intended to be delivered in standardized modular units. Over $150 million has been invested in the development and demonstration of the STG+® technology since 2007, including the construction and operation of a demonstration plant that has completed over 10,000 hours of operation.
