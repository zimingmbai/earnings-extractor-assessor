Atossa Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides a Corporate Update

SEATTLE, WASHINGTON, November 10, 2026 — Atossa Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: ATOS) (Atossa or the Company), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing novel therapies in oncology and other areas of high unmet clinical need, today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 and provided an update on recent corporate developments.

 

“In recent weeks, we took meaningful steps to enable shareholders to participate in potential future value from our rare disease programs,” stated Steven C. Quay, M.D., Ph.D., Atossa's Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer. “Our recently announced contingent value rights agreement entitles holders to share in 25% of the net proceeds, up to $50 million, from the sale of the first priority review voucher awarded to Atossa from its rare disease development programs. At the same time, we continued to advance (Z)-endoxifen toward several near-term milestones, including anticipated FDA clearance of our IND applications in Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy and McCune-Albright Syndrome in early 2027. In December 2026, presentations at the San Antonio Breast Cancer Symposium will include Karisma follow-up results and selected data from the ongoing I-SPY2 study evaluating (Z)-endoxifen in combination with other therapies.”

 

Third Quarter 2026 & Recent Highlights

 

•
Stapled Contingent Value Rights (CVR) Agreement

 

o
On October 8, 2026, the Company entered into a Stapled Contingent Value Rights Agreement (the CVR Agreement), and the Board of Directors (the Board) declared a dividend of one CVR for each share of Atossa common stock outstanding as of October 19, 2026 and issued thereafter. The CVRs remain attached to the underlying shares and will not trade separately unless the Board elects to detach them.

 

o
CVR holders will be entitled, in the aggregate, to 25% of the net proceeds from the sale or other monetization of the first qualifying U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) priority review voucher (PRV) awarded to Atossa, subject to a maximum aggregate payment of $50 million.

 

o
No PRV has been awarded to the Company, and there can be no assurance that Atossa will receive or monetize a PRV or that any payment will ultimately be made under the CVR Agreement.

 

•
CFO Attendance at Global Genes RARE Drug Development Symposium
o
Mark Daniel, Atossa’s Chief Financial Officer, attended the Global Genes RARE Drug Development Symposium, held September 9–11, 2026, in Boston, Massachusetts. The symposium brought together patient advocates, researchers and biopharmaceutical industry leaders to explore research strategies, clinical trial readiness and collaboration in rare disease drug development. Atossa’s attendance supported its efforts to understand patient community priorities and explore approaches to advancing its rare disease development programs.

 

•
CEO Attendance at NORD Rare Diseases and Orphan Products Breakthrough Summit
o
Steven C. Quay, M.D., Ph.D., Atossa’s Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer, attended the National Organization for Rare Disorders (NORD) Rare Diseases and Orphan Products Breakthrough Summit, held October 25–27, 2026, in Washington, D.C. The Summit brought together patient advocates, researchers, industry leaders and policymakers to discuss advances in rare disease research, drug development, patient engagement and access to treatments. Atossa’s attendance supported its continued engagement with the rare disease community as the Company advances its (Z)-endoxifen programs in Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy (DMD) and McCune-Albright Syndrome (MAS).

 

Anticipated Milestones

 

o
The Company anticipates Investigational New Drug (IND) application clearance from the FDA for (Z)-endoxifen in MAS and DMD by early 2027, with initiation of Phase 2 clinical trials expected in 2027.

 

 

 

o
The Karisma trial was completed and the results finalized in the clinical study report. The Company is presenting these data at the San Antonio Breast Cancer Symposium (SABCS) in December 2026, and will publicize the findings thereafter.

 

o
The Company anticipates topline data from the Phase 2 EVANGELINE study of (Z)-endoxifen plus goserelin as neoadjuvant therapy in premenopausal women with ER+/HER2- breast cancer in the second quarter of 2027. Enrollment for the study was completed during the second quarter of 2026.

 

o
Enrollment in the (Z)-endoxifen plus elagolix and (Z)-endoxifen plus abemaciclib arms of the Phase 2 I-SPY2 study is complete, and select data will be presented at SABCS in December 2026.

About Atossa Therapeutics

Atossa Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: ATOS) is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing innovative medicines in oncology and other areas of significant unmet need. The Company’s lead product candidate, (Z)-endoxifen, is currently in development across several clinical settings.

(Z)-Endoxifen is a potent Selective Estrogen Receptor Modulator/Degrader (SERM/D) with demonstrated activity across multiple mechanisms of interest. Atossa is evaluating its potential applications in oncology and rare diseases. The Company’s proprietary oral formulation has shown a favorable safety profile and pharmacology distinct from tamoxifen, including ER-targeted effects and PKC inhibition. Atossa’s (Z)-endoxifen is not approved for any indication.

Atossa has received Orphan Drug Designation from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for (Z)-endoxifen for the treatment of DMD, as well as Rare Pediatric Disease (RPD) designation for (Z)-endoxifen for the treatment of both DMD and MAS. Upon approval of a qualifying marketing application, drugs with RPD designation may be eligible for a Priority Review Voucher (PRV), which can be used to obtain priority review for a future application or may be sold or transferred to another sponsor. In the last 18–24 months, disclosed PRV sales have ranged from $100–$220 million.

Atossa’s (Z)-endoxifen program is supported by a growing global intellectual property portfolio, including multiple recently issued U.S. patents and numerous pending applications worldwide.

More information is available at https://atossatherapeutics.com.
