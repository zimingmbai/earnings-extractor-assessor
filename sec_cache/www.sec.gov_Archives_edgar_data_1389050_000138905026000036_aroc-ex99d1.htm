Archrock Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

​

HOUSTON, November 2, 2026 - Archrock, Inc. (NYSE: AROC) (“Archrock” or the “Company”) today reported results for the third quarter 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

●Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $370.8 million compared to $382.4 million in the third quarter of 2025.
●Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $84.5 million and EPS was $0.48 compared to $71.2 million and $0.40, respectively, in the third quarter of 2025.
●Adjusted net income (a non-GAAP measure defined below) for the third quarter of 2026 was $84.7 million and adjusted EPS (a non-GAAP measure defined below) was $0.49, compared to $73.2 million and $0.42, respectively, in the third quarter of 2025.
●Adjusted EBITDA (a non-GAAP measure defined below) for the third quarter of 2026 was $228.4 million compared to $220.9 million in the third quarter of 2025.
●Declared a quarterly dividend of $0.23 per common share for the third quarter of 2026, approximately 10% higher compared to the third quarter of 2025, resulting in dividend coverage of 3.8x.
●Leverage ratio of 2.5x as of September 30, 2026, down from 3.1x as of September 30, 2025.
●Reaffirmed full-year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance of $865 million to $885 million.

​

Management Commentary and Outlook

​

“The continued strength and profitability of our business reflect consistent customer demand and a structurally tight compression market,” said Brad Childers, Archrock’s President and Chief Executive Officer. “We achieved our strongest bookings quarter since the second quarter of 2023, including strong bookings in the Permian Basin. Rising gas-to-oil ratios and additional takeaway capacity in the basin are supporting sustained compression requirements. While customer maintenance deferrals affected aftermarket activity and higher lube oil prices created near-term cost pressure, we are actively mitigating these headwinds through cost discipline and operating efficiencies. The underlying demand fundamentals remain strong, supporting our confidence in the long-term outlook.

“We remain focused on executing our disciplined growth program, deploying horsepower to meet customer demand and delivering the operational reliability our customers expect. With durable natural gas demand supported by long-term growth in LNG exports and power generation, a strong balance sheet and a disciplined, returns-based approach to capital deployment, Archrock is well positioned to invest alongside our customers at attractive returns, generate durable free cash flow and deliver consistent shareholder returns,” concluded Childers.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Archrock’s third quarter 2026 net income of $84.5 million included a non-cash long-lived and other asset impairment of $3.2 million. Archrock’s third quarter 2025 net income of $71.2 million included a non-cash long-lived and other asset impairment of $4.7 million, transaction-related costs totaling $1.8 million, and restructuring charges of $0.7 million.

Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 and 2025 included $2.1 million and $3.8 million, respectively, in net gains primarily related to the sale of compression and other assets. Also included in Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter of 2025 was a net benefit to contract operations cost of sales of $9.9 million as a result of sales and use tax audit settlements.

Contract Operations

For the third quarter of 2026, contract operations segment revenue totaled $331.2 million, an increase of 1.5% compared to $326.3 million in the third quarter of 2025. Total operating horsepower at the end of the third quarter of 2026 was 4.5 million compared to 4.7 million at the end of the third quarter of 2025, reflecting period-end fleet utilization of 94.3% and the sale of approximately 112,000 non-strategic operating horsepower since the prior-year period.

​

Adjusted gross margin for the third quarter of 2026 was $238.7 million, compared to $239.6 million in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted gross margin percentage for the third quarter of 2026 was 72%, compared to 73% in the third quarter of 2025. Excluding the aforementioned $9.9 million tax benefit for the third quarter of 2025, adjusted gross margin was $229.7 million and adjusted gross margin percentage was 70%.

​

Aftermarket Services

For the third quarter of 2026, aftermarket services segment revenue totaled $39.7 million, compared to $56.2 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily reflecting reduced customer demand for major maintenance service activity and lower parts sales. Adjusted gross margin for the third quarter of 2026 was $9.1 million, compared to $13.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted gross margin percentage for the third quarter of 2026 was 23%, compared to 23% for the third quarter of 2025.

Balance Sheet

Long-term debt was $2.3 billion, and our available liquidity totaled $637 million at September 30, 2026. Our leverage ratio was 2.5x as of September 30, 2026, down from 3.1x as of September 30, 2025.

Shareholder Returns

Quarterly Dividend

Our Board of Directors recently declared a quarterly dividend of $0.23 per share of common stock, or $0.92 per share on an annualized basis, approximately 10% higher compared to the third quarter of 2025. Dividend coverage in the third quarter of 2026 was 3.8x. The third quarter 2026 dividend will be paid on November 10, 2026 to stockholders of record at the close of business on November 3, 2026.

Share Repurchase Program

No shares were repurchased during the third quarter of 2026. As of September 30, 2026, $113.2 million remained available under the share repurchase program.

Since the inception of the share repurchase program in April 2023 through September 30, 2026, we have repurchased 4,632,263 shares of common stock for an aggregate purchase price of $96.9 million, or an average price of $20.91 per share.

About Archrock

Archrock is an energy infrastructure company with a primary focus on midstream natural gas compression and a commitment to helping its customers produce, compress and transport natural gas in a safe and environmentally responsible way. Headquartered in Houston, Texas, Archrock is a premier provider of natural gas compression services to customers in the energy industry throughout the U.S. and a leading supplier of aftermarket services to customers that own compression equipment. For more information on how Archrock embodies its purpose, WE POWER A CLEANER AMERICA®, visit www.archrock.com.
