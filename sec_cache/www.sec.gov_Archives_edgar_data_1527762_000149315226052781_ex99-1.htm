Chaince Digital Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results: Revenue Up 92% Year-Over-Year, Shareholders’ Equity at Highest Level in Nearly Eight Years

 

Nov 17, 2026

 

Underwriting and brokerage drove growth across a diversified financial services platform and the Company reported net income for the quarter. The Company held approximately $60.0 million in cash, short-term investments and stablecoins at quarter-end

 

NEW YORK, NY, Nov. 17, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Chaince Digital Holdings Inc. (Nasdaq: CD) (“Chaince Digital” or the “Company”), a digital finance and technology company focused on tokenization, on-chain innovation, and regulated brokerage services, today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Revenue growth for the first nine months of 2026 was driven by underwriter services, industry advisory and consulting, and transaction execution and brokerage, reflecting an expansion in both the number of engagements and the diversity of services provided compared with 2025. Nine-month revenue of $2.78 million has already exceeded the Company’s full-year 2025 revenue of $1.87 million by approximately 49%. Alongside this growth, the Company strengthened its capital position and simplified its capital structure by repurchasing and canceling all of the outstanding warrants issued in its November 2023 private placement. The Company also continued to expand its capital markets platform and invest in tokenization development.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

 

●Total revenues were $1.81 million, up 92.0% from $0.94 million in the third quarter of 2025.
   
●Underwriter services generated $1.35 million, or approximately 74% of quarterly revenue, and led growth in the quarter.
   
●Gross profit was $1.39 million, up 83.3% year-over-year, with a gross margin of 76.7%.
   
●Operating loss narrowed 43.6% year-over-year.
   
●Net income was $0.23 million, compared with a net loss of $2.55 million in the third quarter of 2025. Net income for the quarter included fair value gains on stablecoins and digital assets and interest and dividend income.

 

Nine Months 2026 Financial Highlights

 

●Total revenues were $2.78 million, up 131.4% from $1.20 million in the first nine months of 2025.
   
●Industry advisory and consulting revenue increased 137.6% year-over-year.
   
●Net loss narrowed 58.7% year-over-year.
   
●Client base grew approximately 60% year-over-year.
   
●Net cash used in operating activities of continuing operations declined 43.0% year-over-year.

 

Balance Sheet

 

●Cash, short-term investments and stablecoins totaled approximately $60.0 million as of September 30, 2026, compared with approximately $39.0 million as of December 31, 2025.
   
●Total shareholders’ equity was $61.7 million, up 40.1% from $44.0 million as of December 31, 2025. Total liabilities represented approximately 6% of total assets.

 

Business and Strategic Highlights

 

●Capital markets platform. Through Chaince Securities, LLC, its FINRA-registered broker-dealer subsidiary, the Company provides investment banking, transaction execution, brokerage-related and other business consulting services to companies pursuing securities offerings in the U.S. capital markets, as well as investment-related solutions to institutional investors, high-net-worth individuals and emerging issuers. Underwriter services, transaction execution and brokerage services, and clearing-related brokerage services each contributed to third-quarter revenue.
   
●Tokenization development. The Company continued to invest in the development of its tokenization platform network, including blockchain architecture and a real-world asset tokenization framework.

 

 
 

 

●Registered direct offering. On August 11, 2026, the Company closed a registered direct offering of 30,560,000 ordinary shares for gross proceeds of approximately $16.2 million.
   
●Warrant repurchase. The Company repurchased and canceled all of the outstanding warrants issued in its November 2023 private placement, covering 42,755,344 underlying ordinary shares, for $2.0 million in cash.
   
●Disciplined capital management. The Company has not sold any shares under its at-the-market offering program established in August 2026. As of November 12, 2026, the Company had 110,003,800 ordinary shares outstanding, unchanged from September 30, 2026, and no warrants outstanding.
   
●Filecoin mining wind-down. All mining nodes had ceased operations by September 30, 2026.

 

Management Commentary

 

“The Company delivered encouraging results in the third quarter,” said Shi Qiu, Chief Executive Officer of Chaince Digital. “Revenue nearly doubled year-over-year, as our brokerage and underwriting businesses grew rapidly and drove a significant increase in revenue, and we ended the quarter with a stronger balance sheet, with total shareholders’ equity at its highest level in nearly eight years. That stronger financial position gives management the confidence and flexibility to pursue new business opportunities.”
