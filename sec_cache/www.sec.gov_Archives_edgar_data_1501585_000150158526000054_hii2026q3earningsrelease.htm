HII Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

NEWPORT NEWS, Va. (October 29, 2026) - HII (NYSE: HII) today reported results for the third quarter of fiscal 2026.

Highlights
•Record third quarter revenues of $3.5 billion
•Third quarter net earnings were $217 million or $5.48 diluted earnings per share
•New contract awards in the third quarter totaled $32.4 billion
•Company raises the FY26 shipbuilding revenue guidance range to between $10.5 and $10.7 billion1
•Company reaffirms FY26 shipbuilding operating margin2 guidance range of 6.0 to 6.5 percent1
•Company reaffirms previously issued FY26 free cash flow2 outlook1

Third Quarter Results
Third quarter 2026 revenues of $3.5 billion were up 10.8% from the third quarter of 2025, driven by growth at Newport News Shipbuilding and Ingalls Shipbuilding.

Operating income in the third quarter of 2026 was $246 million and operating margin was 7.0%, compared to $161 million and 5.0%, respectively, in the third quarter of 2025.

Segment operating income2 in the third quarter of 2026 was $262 million and segment operating margin2 was 7.4%, compared to $179 million and 5.6%, respectively, in the third quarter of 2025.

Net earnings in the third quarter of 2026 were $217 million, compared to $145 million in the third quarter of 2025. Diluted earnings per share in the quarter was $5.48, compared to $3.68 in the third quarter of 2025.

Net cash provided by operating activities in the quarter was $520 million and free cash flow2 was $429 million, compared to net cash provided by operating activities of $118 million and free cash flow2 of $16 million in the third quarter of 2025.

New contract awards in the third quarter of 2026 were $32.4 billion. Year-to-date contract awards totaled $43.1 billion.

Commenting on the Company's third quarter 2026 results, Chris Kastner, HII's president and CEO said, “Our third quarter results reflect solid performance across all three segments, and the significant awards we received during the quarter have now secured approximately 90 percent of our expected shipbuilding revenue through 2030. With that visibility, our focus is squarely on shipbuilding execution. At Mission Technologies, we won landmark maritime autonomy awards for the Medium Unmanned Surface Vessel (MUSV) and the Lionfish Small Unmanned Undersea Vehicle (SUUV), demonstrating our continued leadership in unmanned systems and maritime autonomy."

About HII

HII is America's Seapower Company. The nation’s largest shipbuilder, HII builds the manned and unmanned maritime systems and all-domain mission technologies that project U.S. and allied power around the world. With a more than 140-year history of advancing U.S. national security, HII is headquartered in Virginia and employs 46,000 people across three divisions. For more information, visit www.HII.com.

Exhibit B: Non-GAAP Measures Definitions & Reconciliations

This earnings release contains non-GAAP (accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America) financial measures as defined by SEC Regulation G and indicated by a footnote in the text of this release. Definitions for the non-GAAP measures, and related reconciliations, are provided below. Because not all companies use identical definitions or calculations, our presentation of these measures may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies.

Segment Operating Income and Segment Operating Margin. We internally manage our operations by reference to segment operating income and segment operating margin and use these measures to evaluate our core operating performance. We believe that segment operating income and segment operating margin reflect additional ways of viewing aspects of our operations that, when viewed with our GAAP results, provide a more complete understanding of factors and trends affecting our business. These measures should be considered in addition to, and not as alternatives for, operating income and operating margin or any other performance measure presented in accordance with GAAP.

Segment operating income is defined as operating income for the relevant segment(s) before the Operating FAS/CAS Adjustment and non-current state income taxes.

Segment operating margin is defined as segment operating income as a percentage of sales and service revenues.

Shipbuilding operating margin, Mission Technologies EBITDA and Mission Technologies EBITDA margin. We use shipbuilding operating margin, Mission Technologies EBITDA and Mission Technologies EBITDA margin to evaluate our core operating performance. We believe these measures reflect additional ways of viewing aspects of our operations that, when viewed with our GAAP results, provide a more complete understanding of factors and trends affecting our business. These measures should be considered in addition to, and not as alternatives for, operating income and operating margin or any other performance measure presented in accordance with GAAP.

Shipbuilding operating margin is defined as the combined segment operating income of our Newport News Shipbuilding segment and Ingalls Shipbuilding segment as a percentage of shipbuilding revenue. Shipbuilding revenue is the sum of revenues of our Newport News Shipbuilding segment and Ingalls Shipbuilding segment.

Mission Technologies EBITDA is defined as Mission Technologies segment operating income before interest expense, income taxes, depreciation, and amortization.

Mission Technologies EBITDA margin is defined as Mission Technologies EBITDA as a percentage of Mission Technologies revenues.

Free cash flow. We use free cash flow as a key operating metric in assessing the performance of our business and as a key performance measure in evaluating management performance and determining incentive compensation. We believe free cash flow is an important liquidity measure that may be useful to investors and other users of our financial statements because it provides insight into our current and period-to-period performance and our ability to generate cash from continuing operations. Free cash flow has limitations as an analytical tool and should not be considered in isolation from, or as a substitute for, net income as a measure of our performance or net cash provided by operating activities as a measure of our liquidity.

Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by (used in) operating activities less capital expenditures net of related grant proceeds.
