IQST - IQSTEL Reports $313.0 Million in Nine-Month Revenue, Up 34.5% on Organic Growth

·       Telecom segment generates $1.31 million in operating income for the first nine months of 2026

·       Stockholders’ equity reaches $17.67 million

NEW YORK, November 16, 2026 — IQSTEL Inc. (NASDAQ: IQST) (“IQSTEL” or the “Company”), a global telecommunications and technology company, today reported results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026. The Company generated $313.0 million in consolidated revenue during the first nine months of 2026, an increase of $80.3 million, or 34.5%, from $232.7 million in the same period of 2025. According to management, the revenue growth was entirely organic.

Consolidated gross profit for the nine-month period increased 19.3% to $7.81 million, from $6.55 million a year earlier.

Third-Quarter Results

Consolidated revenue for the third quarter was $106.0 million, compared with $102.9 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 3.1%. Consolidated gross profit rose 8.9% to $2.98 million, from $2.74 million.

The consolidated operating loss for the quarter was $746,911, reflecting segment results, corporate expenses and eliminations.

Segment Operating Performance

The Telecom segment generated $95.25 million in third-quarter revenue, $657,056 in operating income and $539,203 in segment net income. Nine-month Telecom revenue was $277.62 million, with operating income of $1.31 million and segment net income of $1.06 million.

The Fintech segment posted $11.18 million in third-quarter revenue, with an operating loss of $16,438 and a segment net loss of $16,670. Nine-month Fintech revenue was $37.13 million, with an operating loss of $47,403 and a segment net loss of $48,217.

Balance Sheet

At September 30, 2026, IQSTEL held $2.95 million in cash, compared with $2.16 million at December 31, 2025. Total stockholders’ equity increased 8.5%, from $16.28 million at year-end 2025 to $17.67 million at September 30, 2026.

About IQSTEL Inc.

IQSTEL Inc. (NASDAQ: IQST) is a global telecommunications and technology company operating through two core business divisions: Telecom and Digital Services. The Telecom Division is the foundation of IQSTEL’s global platform, operating across 20 countries with more than 600 telecommunications carrier interconnections and delivering international voice, SMS, messaging, and connectivity solutions to some of the world’s largest telecom operators and enterprise customers. Through these customer relationships, IQSTEL’s platform has the potential to reach approximately 2.3 billion end users worldwide. Building on this global infrastructure and commercial reach, the Digital Services Division is focused on higher-margin technology solutions across Content Services, Artificial Intelligence, Intelligent Communications, Cybersecurity, Fintech, and Enterprise Automation. Built through nearly two decades of organic growth and strategic acquisitions, IQSTEL is leveraging the scale and reach of its Telecom business to accelerate the growth of Digital Services and drive its next phase of revenue and adjusted EBITDA expansion.
